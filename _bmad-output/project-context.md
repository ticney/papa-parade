## Critical Implementation Rules

### Engine-Specific Rules

**Simulation core (`src/sim`) — pure, deterministic, headless**
- Fixed step `STEP_MS = 10` of *sim time*; client loop = rAF + accumulator, max `MAX_STEPS_PER_FRAME = 10`, render interpolated with `alpha`.
- `timeScale` (slow-mo ×0.3 perfect, ×0.2 last threat, ×0.8 instinct) changes how many steps run per real second — never the step size. Every step is always 10 ms of `simTime`. Effective scale = min(base scale set by `SetTimeScale`, every active slow-mo); slow-mos are `World.time.slowMos`, one per source, see `src/sim/time/slowMo.ts`.
- Hitstop is a *real-time freeze*: sim sets `freezeRealMs`, client loop consumes it (no steps, render keeps going). Sim never reads real time; in headless, freeze is a no-op.
- Pause = loop stops stepping; resume after a 3 s real-time countdown. Never "pause" by setting `timeScale = 0`.
- `World` is plain serializable data: no classes, functions, Maps with object keys, or Three.js refs. Systems are `(world, ctx) => void` run in the fixed order in `step.ts` (commands → time → sequence → director → disruptions → baby → threats → papa → physics → gauges → instinct → scoring → flow). Systems never call each other; they read/write `World` and push `SimEvent`s to `ctx.events`. `runGauges` (stories 1.19, 3.1, 4.3) is the fatigue, then the noise, then the need, then the debug `SetNoise` and `SetBladder` (the commanded value is the step's final one). A level opts into fatigue with `gauges.fatigue.microSleepDef`, into noise with `gauges.noise: {}` and into the need with `gauges.need: {}`; `addFatigue` is the only writer of `world.gauges.fatigue`, `addNoise` (with `decayNoise`, the silent 1 %/s fall) of `world.gauges.noisePct`, and `addBladder` of `world.gauges.bladderPct`.
- **The noise gauge (story 3.1).** `feel.noise` (`decayPerSec`, `alertPct`, one row per source in `sources`) and `gauges.noise: {}` in the level. `runNoise`, called by `runGauges` after `runFatigue`, is the one gauge built on events (epics 3.1 AC 3): it decays the value silently (`decayNoise`, no event), then reads **this step's** `PropImpact`, `PhoneRinging` and `BabyCrying` (scanned from the end of `ctx.events` while `t === world.time.sim`, like `isBabyFoundNow`, because `ctx.events` is drained by the caller, not per step) and never the phone's, the props' or the baby's state. Every source goes through `addNoise`, which clamps silently to `[0, 100]` and emits `NoiseChanged{source, noisePct, deltaPct}` only for a real change. A prop that made noise is silent for `samePropCooldownMs` (`world.gauges.propNoiseAt`), so a bounce is one fall. The baby cries through `startBabyCry` (`sim/baby/cry.ts`, called by `gagThreat` when the def has `gagCryMs`) and `runBabyCry` emits one `BabyCrying` per `BABY_CRYING_EVERY_MS`. The client alerts on an upward crossing of `alertPct` read from `NoiseChanged` (`isNoiseAlertCrossing`): the client event `NoiseAlertReached` (haptic cue `noise_alert`) and the `pp-hud-gauge-alert` shake. At 100 % in a level with noise, Mama wakes (story 3.2, below). The snore (story 4.4) is a source: `runNoise` reads `PapaSnored` (a sequence's failure) and adds `sources.snore.noisePct`.
- **Mama's note (story 1.22).** `World.score` holds what feeds it: `perfects` (+1 in `resolveThreat` on a `perfect`, the micro-sleep's included) and `nearMisses` (+1 beside the one `NearMiss`, in the stairs gag): counters are written at their source, no system reads `ctx.events`. In the step that ends the level, `runFlow` calls the helper `scoreLevel` right after `LevelEnded`: `computeMamaScore` (pure, `sim/scoring/`) reads the counters, `world.mess` (props on the floor, broken props, `mischiefs`) and `feel.mama` (weights, maxima, `starThresholdPoints`), clamps and rounds each row, writes `world.score.final` and emits `LevelScored` (same `t`). The world is frozen from then on, so the note matches the room left on screen. `MamaSchema` makes the maxima (whole numbers) add up to 100 and `baseScorePoints` equal sigh max + scream max, so the four rows always add up. `runScoring` is the combo's carry tick only (story 4.2, below). The client stops the loop on `LevelScored` (`levelSession.finish()`, the sim stays readable) and enters `result`; the Result sequence's timings are `feedback.json › resultScreen` (real ms; `inputGraceMs` makes the screen ignore a press that starts just after it, or its buttons, come up), its stars play the client events `ResultStarStamped` / `ResultLastStarStamped`, whose cues have a `uiElement` visual (the stamped star is the visual twin).
- **Busy Papa (stories 1.19, 1.20, 4.3).** While Papa is `drink`, `asleep`, `onPhone` or `onToilet` (`isPapaBusy`), `runPapa` ignores `TapFloor`, `TapObject`, `Move`, `MoveStop` and `PressThreat`, `runThreats` ignores `PressThreat`, and the client sends none; `Release` still passes. A threat whose def has `target: "papa"` (the micro-sleep, and the cry in the arms of story 4.1) is spawned by the sim, stands on Papa, never counts against a wave's `maxSimultaneous`, is never drawn by the director or locked on by the baby, and a press on it does not glide Papa.
- **Disruptions (story 1.20).** A disruption (the phone) is not a threat: a wave plans it with `disruptions: [{ def, object, atMs }]` (`content/disruptions/<def>.json`, on an object of the level of the same `kind`), `runDisruptions` runs right after the director and the director never counts, draws or locks on it. The phone goes `pending → ringing → answered / timedOut`, once per level, with no PRNG (a phone still ringing when the level ends is closed by `runFlow`: `timedOut` and `PhoneRingEnded{levelEnded}`, so every `PhoneRingStarted` has exactly one end): `PhoneRinging` once per `PHONE_RINGING_EVERY_MS` (the unit of the noise rule: `runNoise` adds `feel.noise.sources.phoneRinging.perSec` for each), and a `TapObject` that arrives while it rings answers it (+`answerFatiguePoints`, `onPhone` for `answerBusyMs`). Its cues come from the def's `feedback`, not from `feedback.json › events`. `StartDisruption` is the debug command.
- **The suspicious silence (story 1.21).** A threat def with `target: "hiddenZone"` (`findMaxMs`, `surpriseGags`) is planned at a `ZONE_hidden_*` of the geometry and is drawn by the director only while the baby is free (`isBabyFree`) and Papa is outside its zone box; while a silence is under way (`isSilenceUnderway`) the director draws nothing else. Picking it does not telegraph it: `hideThreat` puts it `idle → hidden` (`ThreatHidden`), and a `hidden` threat counts as active (`isThreatActive`) but has no window and cannot be pressed. `runSilence`, run first by `runBaby`, takes it (`mischiefSilent`, `world.silence`), crawls to the zone at `sneakSpeedMps`, stops the babble on entering it, emits `SilenceStarted` after `silenceAlertMs`, and then, from the next step on, either the baby is found (Papa's point in the zone box, or a `TapBaby` with a non-busy Papa, wherever Papa stands: `SilenceEnded{found}`, then a normal `telegraphThreat`; the tap that found him does not also walk Papa) or `findMaxMs` runs out (`SilenceEnded{timedOut}`, a `late` gag drawn from `surpriseGags` with one `nextFloat`, `mess.mischiefs + 1`) or the level ends (`SilenceEnded{levelEnded}`, closed by `runFlow`, no mischief). `ForceThreat` (debug) starts any idle threat through the director's helper. The music cut (`audio.setMusicMuted`) and the `silence-bubble` follow `world.silence.startedAt` per frame, never the events; `SilenceStarted` only plays the vibration. Hit-test priority is threat > baby / object, whichever is nearer to the finger (a tie goes to the baby) > floor (`pickBaby`, `TapBaby`); outside a started silence `TapBaby` takes the baby (story 4.1, see « Carrying the baby »).
- **Carrying the baby (story 4.1).** The baby's state is the single source of truth: `baby.state === 'carried'` (`isBabyCarried`); there is no `papa.carrying`. A level opts in with `carry: { armsCryDef? }` (`SimLevel.hasCarry`, `safeZones`, `armsCryIndex`) and needs a `ZONE_safe_*` (the playpen); without `carry`, `TapBaby` only walks Papa to the baby as before. `TapBaby` (`papa/papaSystem.ts`, helpers in `baby/carry.ts`) does, in this order: the tap that found a hiding baby is spent; a carried baby is put down here; a hiding baby is only walked to; a baby within `feel.carry.reachM` is taken at once; otherwise Papa walks to him with `papa.intent = 'takeBaby'` and takes him on arrival if he is then in reach. A `TapFloor` whose walk ends (`goalXM`/`goalZM`, not the tap) inside a safe zone with the baby carried sets `intent = 'putDownBaby'` (put down on arrival, `BabyPutDown{safe}` says whether Papa stands in a safe zone). `intent` is non-`none` only in `walkPath` and every other command, glide or busy state resets it. Taking cancels every telegraphed or open threat of the baby (`target: "anchor"`, and a found silence's `hiddenZone`) with no gag (`ThreatCancelled{reason}` via `cancelThreat`: no result, no rearm, no slow-mo, no hitstop); the ones on Papa stay. A second `TapBaby` in the step that took or put down the baby is ignored (`runPapa`). A carried baby is inert (`runBaby` returns after `runCarry`) and rides Papa's point at the end of `runPapa`; the director draws no threat while he is carried (no draw spent); fatigue grows `fatigue.carriedMultiplier` times as fast; a Firmness pressed while carried is a `wrongResponse` gag. The cry in the arms is a second system-spawned threat on Papa (after the micro-sleep, `world.carry.armsCryAt`, telegraphed by `runCarry` only while carried, with the same « judged before the end » helper as the micro-sleep, `threats/judgeable.ts`): its gag `arms_wail` may not be `papa_asleep`, and it is cancelled by a put-down. The number of `world.rng` draws is fixed (one per take in a level with a cry in the arms, one per put-down, one per cry). The client reads the state: `babyView` draws him lifted in Papa's arms, a tap aims at him there, and a `firm` marker shows `verb.put_down_first` while he is carried.
- **The combo (story 4.2).** `world.combo` (`multiplier` from `feel.combo.minMultiplier`, `basePoints`, `carryTickAt`) and `world.score.comboPoints` are written only by `sim/scoring/combo.ts`, at the source like `world.score.perfects`: `resolveThreat` calls `addResponseToCombo` (+1 multiplier and `basePerResponsePoints`, perfect or success, baby carried or not), `gagThreat` calls `resetCombo(…, 'gag')`, `takeBaby` / `putDownBaby` start and stop the carry tick, and `cancelThreat` changes nothing. Past `maxMultiplier` the multiplier stays (and a `SetFeel` that lowers the maximum never lowers a running multiplier) but the base points still grow. **Every helper is a no-op in a level without `carry`** (no event, nothing written, so the goldens of the levels without it keep their events), and none draws from `world.rng`. `runScoring`, between `runGauges` and `runFlow`, is the one clock-driven part: at `carryTickAt` the combo climbs and the next tick is due `carryTickMs` after that one (no drift); a gag while carried pushes it back. The cash-in is the last thing `putDownBaby` does, after the cry in the arms was cancelled: only on a `safe` drop (Papa's point in a safe zone, the same value as `BabyPutDown.safe`) with a multiplier above the minimum, it emits `ComboCashedIn{points = multiplier × basePoints}` then `ComboChanged{cashIn}` and starts the `comboCashIn` slow-mo (no hitstop); a drop on bare floor neither cashes in nor resets, and a gag in the same step wins (`runThreats` runs first). `computeMamaScore` adds `comboPoints` to the Rire (`perfects × laughPerPerfectPoints + comboPoints`, one rounding, capped), so the note keeps four rows; points not cashed when the level ends are lost. The client reads state, not events, for the badge: `ui/world/comboBadge.ts` (one pooled `CSS2DObject`, text and classes written only when they change, `pp-combo-badge--aside` while a threat that follows Papa is up) shows `world.combo.multiplier` above Papa while it is above the minimum, and `ComboCashedIn` shows « +N » for `feedback.json › comboBadge.cashInHoldMs` of real time; the cue `combo_cashed_in` plays in addition to `baby_put_down`. Mirrors `data-pp-combo` / `data-pp-combo-cashed`. The bots never cash in until the cycle bot (4.10).
- **The pressing need (story 4.3).** `feel.bladder` (`startPct`, `perSec` 0.4, `slowFromPct`, `slowdownPct`, `urgentFromPct`, `toiletDurationMs`) and `gauges.need: {}` in the level (the HUD and level id is `need`, the sim says `bladder`: `gauges/bladder.ts`, `SimLevel.hasBladder`). `addBladder` is the only writer of `world.gauges.bladderPct`: it clamps silently to `[0, 100]`, does nothing (no write, no event) in a level without the gauge, and keeps `isBladderFull` (`bladderPct >= slowFromPct`, recomputed on every call so a `SetFeel` that moves the threshold is followed at the next step) with one `BladderFilled` per upward crossing. `runBladder`, called by `runGauges` after `runNoise`, adds `perSec` for one step and is **frozen while Papa is `onToilet`**; it keeps rising during a coffee, a nap or a call. `bladderSpeedFactor` (`1 − slowdownPct / 100` while full, read from the previous step) multiplies the budget of `walkPath` and `walkDir` in `runPapa` only: **the glide keeps its speed** (`slowdownPct` is held strictly under 100, Papa always arrives). An object of kind `toilet` (`OBJECT_ACTIONS.toilet` → `startToilet`, `onToilet` for `toiletDurationMs`, `ToiletStarted{untilAt}`) needs `gauges.need` and an anchor outside every `hidden` zone (`content:check`: Papa's point stays at the anchor and `silence.ts` would find the baby from there). `finishToilet` drops the need to 0 **at the end** (`ToiletFinished`), like the coffee; `putPapaToSleep` finishes a visit that ends in that very step first, otherwise the nap replaces it (no `ToiletFinished`, no reset). A `TapObject` on the toilet with the baby carried is refused by the sim (`ToiletRefused{objectId}`, no path, no `ObjectTapped`, the current walk goes on), so `onToilet` never coexists with `carried` (the snapshot refuses it). The room goes on without Papa (threats, the baby, silences, the phone, fatigue and noise); no rng is drawn. The client hides the whole of Papa while `onToilet` (`papaView.setAway`, shadow included), gives the combo badge and the hint no pose, warms the door's marker from `urgentFromPct` (one state per object kind in `objectMarkers.update`), dims it while he is in (`pp-object-occupied`), shows `verb.put_down_first` above him for `feedback.json › papaHint.holdMs` (`ui/world/papaHint.ts`), and for Space / A treats a toilet as no object in reach while the baby is carried (so the key puts him down, the next one goes in) and as beating a free baby (`ObjectTarget.kind`). Mirror `data-pp-bladder`, on events only. No bot goes to the toilet (cycle bot: 4.10).
- **Special sequences (story 4.4).** A sequence (`content/sequences/<id>.json`, `SequenceDefSchema`, listed by a level's `sequences`) is a short stretch of play with its own rules: « Rester éveillé » (`stay_awake`) first, then 4.5-4.9. Nothing schedules one in a normal level: `StartSequence{sequenceId}` (journaled, the debug twin of `StartDisruption`; the overlay's « Start », `__PP__.send`, `npm run sim -- --sequence <id> --sequence-at-ms <n>`) does, until the bedtime level (4.10). `FlowState` is `'playing' | 'sequence' | 'ended'`: **every reader of `flow.state` is classified**: `isFlowLive(world)` (`!== 'ended'`) is what the phone, the bots' `playGame`, the autoplay, the client's input and its per-frame audio read; `runFlow` ends the level (timer, noise) in a sequence too; `runFatigue` and `runCarry` keep `=== 'playing'` (a sequence suspends the normal micro-sleep and the cry in the arms). `runSequence` runs right after `advanceTime`, before the director: this step's `StartSequence`s, then the step change (`SequenceStepStarted`, no draw), the current step's handler (`Record<SequenceStepKind, …>`, `sequenceSteps.ts`) and the end test (at `endsAt` with the micro-sleep instance not active: `SequenceEnded{succeeded}`). `startSequence` (refused with no event by `sequenceRefusal`: `running`, `ended`, `papaBusy`, `silence`) stops Papa, cancels every threat announced or open, the ones on Papa too (`ThreatCancelled{sequence}`), takes the baby wherever he is if `babyCarried` (one draw for the cry in the arms, as ever), writes `world.sequence` (`id` non-null exactly while `flow.state === 'sequence'`; `lastId`, `startedAt`, `endedAt` stay) and emits `SequenceStarted`, `SequenceStepStarted{0}`. The `microSleeps` step reuses the level's own micro-sleep instance (`microSleepIndex`) on a cadence of its own (one draw per start and per telegraph, never `gauges.microSleepAt`), and telegraphs only what can be judged before **both** the sequence's `endsAt` and the level's end (`isJudgeableBeforeEnd(…, endMs)`). Suspended: the director (the waves still enter on time: `WaveStarted` and its draw), `ForceThreat`, a pending phone and `StartDisruption` (a ringing phone keeps ticking and times out). Papa stands still: `runPapa` ignores every command but `PressThreat`. A gag ends the sequence (`failSequenceOnGag`, called last by `gagThreat`, `sequences/sequenceEnd.ts`, which imports no threat code): `PapaSnored`, `BabyWoke` (events and cues, no baby state and no cry: D4), `SequenceEnded{failed}`, flow back to `playing`; the snore is `feel.noise.sources.snore` (+15 %, read by `runNoise` from `PapaSnored`) and **belongs to the sequence**: a missed micro-sleep outside one is the plain 3 s nap. The level ending in a sequence closes it first (`SequenceEnded{levelEnded}`, no resume). After a success or a failure `resumeAfterSequence` pushes `director.nextAt`, `gauges.microSleepAt` and `carry.armsCryAt` (when non-null) to at least `now + feel.sequences.resumeGapMs`, with no draw; the baby stays carried, so the director draws nothing until he is put down. A sequence earns nothing of its own: it pays through the combo and the perfects. The client reads **state, every frame**, never events, for the presentation: the sequence bar (`ui/hud/sequenceBar.ts`, two DOM nodes to fit `HUD_MAX_DOM_NODES`: the label is a `::before`, the fill the track's background; mounted while shown and `feedback.sequenceBar.slideMs` of real time after, never on `transitionend`), the compact timer (`timerHud.setCompact`, the same condition), the camera framing (`cameraFx.update(…, framing)` composes with the perfect's move, `sequenceFramingBlendAt` from the world's slice and the sim clock) and the music (`audio.setSequenceMusic(id)` plus `setMusicMuted`: the level stems give way). Only the cues come from events (`sequenceCueOf`, named by the def). The input mapper sends no floor, object or baby tap, no move and no tap marker while `flow.state === 'sequence'`, and `resumeHeld()` runs on `SequenceEnded`. Mirrors `data-pp-flow` (`sequence`), `data-pp-sequence`, `data-pp-sequence-outcome`, `data-pp-sequence-music`.
- **The lullaby walk (story 4.5, snapshot version 21).** « Les 100 pas en rythme » is a `beats` step (`content/sequences/lullaby_walk.json`, the architecture's `sim/sequences/lullabyWalk.ts`) with a `babyAwake` failure and a `calmLeft` bar; the schema refuses one without the other, and `sequenceBeatProblems` (`sim/sequences/beatClock.ts`, shared by `content:check` and `simLevelOf`, same words) refuses an unknown `track`, a decoy with no next beat, a `windowMs` not below the shortest beat interval, no judged beat at all. **The timeline** is `beatTimelineOf(step)` (pure, no zod): the first note at `leadInMs`, the next one `Math.round(MS_PER_MINUTE / bpmAt(previous))` later (the tempo goes linearly from `bpmStart` at the first note to `bpmEnd` at `durationMs`, whole ms), a note exists only if `offsetMs + windowMs <= durationMs`, the first `countInBeats` are heard only, a decoy sits halfway between judged beats `n` and `n + 1`. `simLevelOf` builds it once per step (`SimSequence.beatTimelines`, not in the snapshot); the scheduler, the ring and the bots call the same function. **Two cursors** in `world.sequence.beat` (`cueIndex`, `judgeIndex`, entries of the timeline), kept apart on purpose: `BeatCued` / `DecoyCued` fire at a note's time and `BeatHit` / `BeatMissed` settle a judged beat, so a beat tapped early is still announced at its time. `TapBeat{t}` (journaled, no `threatId`: a beat is not a threat, D2) goes through `classifyTap` on **its own `t`**: the window is `effectiveWindow` (tier × fatigue, computed when the cursor moves, so an announced window keeps its length) with `bufferMs` forced to 0; a tap before the first judged beat is free; the nearer centre of the beat and the decoy before it wins, a tie goes to the beat; a hit settles the beat, a late tap settles it as a miss, anything else is a stray. A miss is the step after `beatAt + windowMs / 2 + toleranceMs + input.lagMs`. Each miss, decoy and stray adds its `…WakePct` to `beat.wakePct` (`BabyStirred`); at 100, `BabyWoke`, `SequenceEnded{failed}` (the end shared with a gag, `failSequence`), no snore, no noise, the baby stays carried. The step draws nothing from `world.rng`; a step earns nothing (D6). `failSequenceOnGag` ends only a `gag` sequence; the end test of `runSequence` is the step handler's `isSettled`. **The client's scheduler** (`client/audio/beatScheduler.ts`) plans each note on the audio clock with `playOneShotAt` from the loop's continuous clock (`GameLoop.clockMs`, `simPerRealMs`, never `world.time.sim`): `audioAtS(beatAt, clockMs, simPerRealMs, audioNowS, leadMs)`; it plans what is within `scheduleAheadMs`, **re-plans every frame a note that has not sounded** (off by more than `replanToleranceMs`: cancelled and planned again, and one beyond the horizon after a slow-down is cancelled), cancels everything while `simPerRealMs` is 0, retries a refused plan and drops a note that is late by more than `scheduleAheadMs`: that one rule covers a slow-mo, a hitstop, a pause and a debug time scale. **`audioOffsetMs` is one correction, not two (D1):** the inputs keep `visualOffsetMs` in `inputMapper.toSim` (and `SetInputLag`), untouched; the notes play `leadMs = audioOffsetMs − visualOffsetMs` earlier. Never also subtract `audioOffsetMs` from the taps: the latency would count twice. The architecture's « les séquences rythmiques utilisent `audioOffsetMs` » is met by this. The input mapper sends `TapBeat` (touch anywhere, Space, A; no marker, no release) while `isBeatStepRunning(world)`, after the threat check. The ring (`ui/world/beatRing.ts`) is driven each frame by the timeline and the loop's clock, never by events; a decoy's is dashed with a ✕; reduced motion drops its scale. `BeatCued` and `DecoyCued` vibrate `light`, so a muted phone follows the tempo. Mirrors `data-pp-beat-hits`, `data-pp-beat-misses`, `data-pp-baby-wake`; `__PP__.beatLog()` (the notes that sounded and the (audio, game clock) pairs). Bots: `perfect` taps each `nextBeatAt`, `human` around it with no reaction floor, `random` taps at its rate in a beats step only (its draws elsewhere are unchanged), `idle` nothing.
- **Two ways to end a level (stories 1.18, 3.2).** `runFlow` ends the level in one private `endLevel`. The timer (`world.time.sim >= durationMs`) gives `LevelEnded{reason: 'timer'}`. Noise at 100 % (`world.gauges.noisePct >= GAUGE_MAX_POINTS`, read after `runGauges`, only in a level with `gauges.noise`, `debug` source included) gives `MamaWoke`, then `LevelEnded{reason: 'mamaWoke'}`, at the same `t`; the noise is checked first, so a tie goes to Mama. Both close a sequence under way (story 4.4), a ringing phone and an open silence first and then score the level. A woken Mama costs stars, not points: `computeMamaScore(tally, mama, isMamaWoke)` takes `min(feel.mama.earlyWakePenaltyStars, baseStars)` off the stars (never below 0, `MamaScore.lostStars`, `LevelScored.lostStars`). The Result screen shows the header « Maman s'est réveillée ! » (client event `ResultMamaWokeShown`) and stamps each lost star last, dashed with a « −1 » chip (`ResultStarLost`). `LEVEL_END_REASONS` types the report's `endsByReason`. `SetNoise` is applied after the step's decay, so the commanded value is the step's final one.
- The wave director (`sim/director`) picks the next threat from `world.rng` among the idle threats of the waves started so far (waves are cumulative); `World.flow` ends the level at the level's `durationMs` (`LevelEnded`), after which a step runs only `applyCommands` and `advanceTime` (`ENDED_SYSTEMS`) so the clock still advances. The last success of a closed wave also starts the `lastThreat` slow-mo (`isLastOfWave`). The level file holds `variant`, `durationMs`, `waves[]` and `feelOverrides` (merged by the client and the CLI before `createSim`).
- Randomness only from `world.rng` (seeded sfc32). IDs only from `world.nextId++`.
- State machines: `state` is a string-literal union + `enteredAt` (sim time). Change state only via `enter…State()`, which emits the event. Measure durations as `world.time.sim - enteredAt`, never frame counters.
- Rapier lives beside `World` behind `PhysicsPort`; only `physics/rapierPhysics.ts` imports Rapier. Rapier = free props only; papa and baby are kinematic on the nav grid. Gameplay consequences come from contact events filtered by impulse threshold. Props are mirrored every step into `World.props` (poses, `isBroken`, `isOnFloor`) and `World.mess`; the Rapier state rides in `SimSnapshot.physics` (base64); `Sim.dispose()` frees it; the room is closed (floor, walls, ceiling) and a push is clamped to `feel.physics.maxPushImpulseNs`. Sim never loads `.glb` — it reads `<level>.geometry.json`.

**Time & input judgement (ADR-002/003/010)**
- Timing windows are in **sim time** (slow-mo stretches them). Calibration offset is applied in **real time**, then converted via `client/timeMap.ts` (2 s ring buffer of `(realTime, simTime)` pairs; older timestamps clamp with a `warn`).
- **The calibration offset (story 2.7) is applied in `client/input/inputMapper.ts` only, to every real stamp it turns into sim time: presses, releases (a corrected press with a raw release would add the offset to every hold), keys, the pad's edge stamps and the stick's `now`.** One local `toSim(active, atReal)` does it, reading `visualOffsetMs()` at each input (0 for a player who has not calibrated: nothing moves). `simClockStamp` (the sim's own clock after a resume) is not an input stamp and stays raw. A negative offset puts `t` ahead of the clock by at most `-offsetMinMs`; `computeApplyAt` and the time map's extrapolation cover it.
- **`SetInputLag { t, lagMs }` (story 2.7 D1, journaled, `World.input.lagMs`, 0 by default, in the snapshot since version 15):** a stamp corrected into the past reaches the sim `lagMs` after the instant it names, so the sim waits that long before it decides what no command has come to say: `classifyPress` calls a press with no release a Firmness only `holdMinMs + lagMs` after its `t`, a window nobody answered gags at `close + tolerance + lagMs`, and a held Firmness resolves at the end of the attack `+ lagMs`. The branch with a release is exact (both stamps are corrected alike), `judgePress` is untouched, and no other system reads a command's `t`. The client sends it at a level's start when `max(0, visualOffsetMs) > 0` (never at 0: goldens, bots and journals gain no entry; never to an autoplay sim) and again, whatever the value, when a recalibration ends with a level active.
- **`SetDifficultyTier { t, tier }` (story 2.8 D1, journaled, `World.difficultyTier`, `'normal'` by default, in the snapshot since version 19):** which entry of `feel.difficulty` (`assistance`, `normal`, `experienced`; ids in `shared/constants.ts`) the windows and the instinct read. `activeDifficulty(world, ctx)` (`src/sim/timing/difficulty.ts`) is the only reader of `feel.difficulty` in the sim (so a level's `feelOverrides` and a live `SetFeel` on the active tier both apply); `telegraphThreat` and `isJudgeableBeforeEnd` use it, and Fatherly Instinct (2.9) must read `hasInstinct` through it. `effectiveWindow` is untouched (the tier is already its first factor). A window already telegraphed keeps its length. The client sends it at a level's start only, when the saved tier is not `normal` and it is not an autoplay run (so goldens, bots and journals at `normal` gain no entry); `npm run sim -- --tier <id>` sends it at t = 0 for a bot.
- Judge on the *press* timestamp: `event.timeStamp - calibration.visualOffsetMs` → `timeMap.realToSim()`. Never judge at `step()` time or on papa's arrival. Papa's glide (≤ `glideMaxMs` 600) is choreography only; the result is known before he arrives.
- Release < `holdMinMs` (400) = Parry, ≥ 400 = Firmness; decided on release or at 400 ms held. No other threshold (`tapMaxMs` is unused).
- Keyboard / gamepad targeting (story 1.17): the client picks the target, never the sim. `client/input/autoTarget.ts` takes the threat whose window closes first (`windowOpenAt + responseWindow.windowMs`, ties to the lower id) inside the cone (`feel.input.autoTargetConeDeg`) around the held move direction from Papa, falling back to the most urgent overall. Space / A with no threat active sends `TapObject{objectId}` for the nearest of `level.objects` within `feel.input.contextReachM`; the sim walks Papa to its anchor and emits `ObjectTapped`. A pad that goes away is a UI flag plus a pause (`requestPause('gamepadDisconnected')`), never a new pause reason, and never blocks a resume.
- Effective window = base × difficulty tier × fatigue mod × instinct mod, then ± hidden tolerance — fixed order, implemented only in `sim/timing/effectiveWindow.ts`.
- Early press follows `feel.earlyPress` (`'fail'` default → gag; `'guard'` → no judgement). Exception: `wait` threats always gag on early press.
- "Do nothing" / "Wait" responses are judged on the *absence* of commands **targeting that threatId** — commands on other threats/floor/objects must not trigger a fail. `perfect` exists for `wait`, not for `nothing`.
- The sim only receives typed, timestamped `SimCommand`s (`PressThreat`, `Release`, `TapFloor`, `TapObject`, `Swipe`, `SetFeel`…). Every command targets one explicit `threatId`; target selection (hit-test priority threat > object > floor, auto-target for keyboard/gamepad) happens in the client — the sim never guesses.

**Content & feel**
- No GDD number hard-coded anywhere. Tunables live in `content/feel.json` (per-level `feelOverrides`), content in `content/**.json`, all validated by Zod schemas in `src/content/schemas/` (types via `z.infer` — don't hand-write duplicate types).
- Only `loadContent()` reads content JSON; the sim gets a `ContentBundle` in `createSim()` and the effective `Feel` lives in `world.feel` (snapshotted, snapshot version 21; `ctx.feel` is a getter onto it). Never `import x from '../../content/…json'`.
- `null` in a threat def (e.g. `windowMs: null`) means "use the `feel.json` default".
- Live tuning goes through the `SetFeel{patch, t}` command (journaled), never by mutating `ctx.feel` directly.
- New threat = new JSON (+ maybe a gag), never a new class. Cross-references (threat→anchor, threat→feedback key, gag ids) must pass `content:check`.
- A gag's sim consequences (near miss, pushed prop) live in `sim/threats/gags.ts` (`applyGag`, one entry per `GagId`); its presentation in `client/render/gags.ts` (`GAG_PRESENTATIONS`); a threat's marker glyph is its def's `icon` (`THREAT_ICONS`, one glyph per entry in `threatMarkers.ts`). Its verb is the def's `verbKey` (a `verb.` key of `strings.fr.json`, `null` only for a `dodge` response), its shape and colour are `iconShape` + `color` and are a pair (`circle` + `parry`, `triangle` + `dodge`, NFR8); the verb is part of the marker, fitted to `VERB_MAX_WIDTH_PCT` of the layer and never under `VERB_MIN_FONT_PX`, and the verbs of simultaneous threats are offset in `worldUi.render` (urgency: `client/input/urgency.ts`, shared with the auto-target).

**Feedback, rendering, UI**
- Sim emits domain events (PascalCase past tense, `type` discriminant, `t` in sim time). Client drains them after each step → `FeedbackDirector` → `content/feedback.json`. Every entry with `audio` MUST also have `visual` and `haptic`. Time-altering effects (hitstop, slow-mo) are decided in the sim, not in feedback.
- Response sound + haptic fire at judgement; final pose animation plays at papa's arrival (`arriveAt`).
- A cue may `layers` extra sounds played right after its `audio` (the baby's laugh on a perfect: one cue = one moment, no ring or haptic of its own per layer). A threat's success/perfect clip is exactly `resp_<response>`, its telegraph a `tel_*` clip no other telegraph shares, its gag a `gag_*` clip (`content:check`, story 2.3). The routing from a sim event to its cues is `client/feedback/simEventFeedback.ts` (pure, unit-tested); the debug overlay's « Sons » folder auditions any cue.
- A cue may `flash` the screen (the perfect's mark; real time, a border glow under reduced motion, never faster than 3 Hz: `SCREEN_FLASH_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`); a sound wave may `pulses` (2 or 3 rings on the sim clock). Telegraph and ring cues vibrate `light`, perfect cues `heavy` and flash, a success never flashes (`content:check`, story 2.4); telegraphs are told apart by their waves, not their vibration. `__PP__.cueLog()` records each cue with the visuals it drew.
- A cue may name an `fx` preset (`feel.fx.success|perfect|gag`: real-time shake and pooled particles, the perfect's stamp; `content:check` pins them on threat cues). The perfect's camera move is `render/cameraFx.ts`, driven per frame by the `perfect` slow-mo (sim clock). Every effect takes `isMotionReduced` from one boot function (story 2.10 plugs the setting there).
- The level music is two synchronized stems (`feedback.json › loops.music.stems`) mixed by `client/audio/adaptiveMusic.ts` from the world's state each frame: the tense stem while a threat is `telegraph|windowOpen`, a low-pass and a lower `playbackRate` while `world.time.scale` is under `feel.music.slowMo.belowScale`, a cut while a silence lasts. Tunables are `feel.music`.
- Rendering: `MeshToonMaterial` (3-tone ramp) + inverted-hull outlines; no heavy post-processing. Views are reconciled from `World` each frame by a `ViewRegistry` with pools.
- UI is vanilla DOM + CSS (`pp-` prefixed kebab-case classes); world-anchored labels via `CSS2DRenderer`. No WebGL UI, no framework.
- **UX spec is the source of truth for the interface:** `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-papa-parade-2026-10-26/DESIGN.md` (tokens, components) and `EXPERIENCE.md` (flows, states, strings, inputs, accessibility). `tokens.css` mirrors the DESIGN.md frontmatter 1:1 (test-enforced); never hard-code a colour, size or UI string — strings live in `content/strings.fr.json` and go through `t(key)`. Reduced motion is client-only: it must never change sim timing (hitstop, slow-mo, windows).
- Audio: hand-rolled Web Audio (buses music/sfx/voice/ui, pre-decoded buffers, unlock on first tap). The baby's babble must loop continuously while `baby.babbling` — its stop *is* the silence telegraph.

### Performance Rules

- **Targets:** 60 FPS stable on iPhone 12 / 2021 Android over a full 5-min level; frame ≤ 16.6 ms with sim ≤ 2 ms (up to 10 steps) and render ≤ 10 ms; tap → visual feedback ≤ 50 ms.
- **Zero allocation in hot paths** (rAF loop, `sim.step()`, systems, view `update`): reuse scratch vectors, no closures/arrays/object literals per frame, no `new Mesh`/`new Vector3` outside pools. `drainEvents()` must not allocate a new array per frame either.
- **Input handlers do almost nothing:** `pointerdown` → timestamp, hit-test, enqueue command, trigger highlight. Visual feedback (highlight + glide start) must appear on the next frame — never wait for the next sim step or papa's arrival.
- **Render budgets:** ≤ 100 draw calls (instance identical props, static furniture merged at export), ≤ 100k on-screen triangles, shared materials, one 256 px palette texture or vertex colors, no texture > 1024 px, blob shadows only (no shadow maps), outlines only on characters / active threats / salient props.
- **DPR:** `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`, stepping down 2 → 1.5 → 1 if mean frame time > 18 ms for 2 s.
- **Loading:** whole level (`.glb` + decoded audio) preloaded behind the loading screen; nothing loads during play. Level payload ≤ 3 MB compressed (< 5 s on 4G); memory ≤ 300 MB JS + GPU.
- **Logging in hot paths:** nothing above `debug`, and guard expensive formatting with `if (log.enabled('debug'))`.
- `renderer.info` is shown in the debug overlay; perf regressions are caught by `tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts` (bot `human`, full level, fails if p95 frame > 20 ms).

**Naming conventions agents get wrong:**
- **Units are mandatory suffixes:** `windowMs`, `glideMaxMs`, `noisePct`, `speedMps`, `fatiguePerSec`. A bare `duration`/`speed` is a review failure.
- **Time instants:** `t` or `…At` = sim time; `…AtReal` = real time (`pressedAtReal`). Use branded `SimMs` / `RealMs` types; never mix them.
- FSM states: camelCase string-literal unions (`'telegraph' | 'windowOpen' | 'resolved'`), never `enum`. Events: PascalCase past tense, subject first (`ThreatTelegraphed`). Commands: PascalCase imperative (`PressThreat`). Booleans: `is`/`has`/`can`. Constants: `UPPER_SNAKE` in `shared/constants.ts` only. Types: PascalCase, no `I` prefix.
- **Blender (`.glb`) nodes:** `COL_<name>` static collider, `NAV_floor` / `NAV_block_<name>`, `ANCHOR_<anchorId>`, `PROP_<kind>_<nn>` (Rapier prop), `ZONE_hidden_|safe_|creak_<name>`; visual meshes unprefixed; lower_snake_case after prefix; meters, Y-up.
- **Audio:** `public/assets/audio/<category>_<name>.m4a`, categories `tel`, `resp`, `gag`, `baby`, `papa`, `env`, `ui`, `music`.

**Errors & logging:**
- I/O (assets, storage, audio unlock, haptics) returns `Result<T, E>` from `@shared/result` (`{ ok: true, value } | { ok: false, error }`) — no library, no throwing.
- Sim invariants use `invariant()` / `clampInvariant(ctx, value, min, max, name)`: throws in dev/test; in prod clamps and emits `InvariantViolated` (client logs `warn`). No `try/catch` in `src/sim`.
- Every fallback emits at least a `warn` — no silent failure. Uncaught errors → global handler → "Oups" screen + level restart.
- Logging: `log.<level>(channel, message, data?)` from `@shared/log`; channels `boot|assets|audio|input|render|feedback|storage|platform|debug|content|sim`. No `console.*` outside `src/shared/log.ts` (lint rule). The sim never logs — it emits events.

### Platform & Build Rules

- **Targets:** browser first (mobile + desktop, playtest by link); iOS/Android via Capacitor in E8. Inputs: touch, keyboard, gamepad — every action must be doable with all three (keyboard/gamepad use auto-targeting of the most urgent threat in the aimed direction, cone `feel.autoTargetConeDeg`).

The settings are rebuilt from the save (`saveStore.current().settings`) by the overlay host, so a screen holds no state of its own; a change is `SettingsChanged { settings }` (volumes follow it, the tier waits for the next game, the vibrations read the save at each pulse).

An unparsable, invalid or future-version save is set aside under `papa-parade.save.invalid` and replaced by defaults, with one `warn` on `storage`; a failed write is a `warn` and the values stay in memory. `boot.ts` awaits `load()` before `appFsm.enter('start')`, so `onStart` stays synchronous inside the gesture.
  - **The calibration flow.** First launch (no `calibration` in the save) goes `start → calibration → loading → level`; `calibrationFlow.startRecalibration(returnTo)` is the one entry (story 2.8's settings row calls it), and `?autoplay` never calibrates. The flow (`client/app/calibrationFlow.ts`) owns the stage and the offsets measured so far, outside the screen: the portrait overlay rebuilds the screen and restarts the current stage only. `data-pp-calibration` (`audio|visual|retry|done`) and `data-pp-calibration-beats` are debug mirrors (`isMirrored` only; `dist:check` forbids the `ppCalibration` marker in a prod build).
- **Build flavors:** `npm run dev` (debug + feel panel + `feel-writer` plugin), `build:playtest` (`VITE_DEBUG_ALLOWED=true`, debug via `?debug=1`, F1 or 3-finger tap), `build` (store/prod: `VITE_DEBUG_ALLOWED=false`). Debug code must sit behind `if (import.meta.env.VITE_DEBUG_ALLOWED)` + dynamic `import()` so it's tree-shaken from store builds (no `window.__PP__`, cheats or panel).
- **`feel-writer` Vite plugin** exists only on the dev server and writes only `content/feel.json` — reject any other path.
- **Static hosting:** `base: './'` (same build works under any path and in Capacitor); strict CSP with `default-src 'self'`, `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'` (Rapier WASM) and `font-src 'self'` (self-hosted woff2); HTTPS. No backend, no network calls, no accounts, no personal data — telemetry is a local journal the tester copies/downloads manually. ### Critical Don't-Miss Rules

- ❌ `Math.random()`, `Date.now()`, `performance.now()`, `setTimeout`, `crypto.randomUUID()` anywhere in `src/sim` (incl. "just for an id" or "just for debug").
- ❌ Importing `three` (even types like `Vector3`) into `src/sim` — use `@shared/math/vec2|vec3`.
- ❌ Reading or mutating `sim.world` from the client to change gameplay — the client only **reads** `world` (readonly) and **writes** via `sim.enqueue(cmd)`. Same for debug cheats and the feel panel (`SetFeel`).
- ❌ Importing from `src/sim/...` deep paths in client/bots — only `@sim`.
- ❌ Hard-coding a GDD value "for now" (window 300, buffer 100, tolerance 60, hitstop 80, glide 600, thresholds…) — put it in `feel.json` even in a prototype.
- ❌ Judging a response when papa *arrives*, or when the command is *processed* by `step()` — judge on the command's `t`.
- ❌ Real-time vs sim-time confusion: calibration in real time, windows in sim time. Slow-mo widens windows on purpose — don't "fix" it.
- ❌ A "do nothing" threat failing because the player tapped the floor or another threat.
- ❌ Adding an `audio` feedback without `visual` + `haptic` (pillar 3; test will fail).
- ❌ Writing `state = 'x'` directly, or counting frames for durations.
- ❌ Non-serializable data in `World` (class instances, closures, `Map`/`Set`, Three/Rapier handles) — breaks snapshots, replay and golden tests.
- ❌ Calling one sim system from another, or reordering `SYSTEMS` without updating golden tests and justifying it.
- ❌ Inventing a threat target in the sim when several are active — the command carries `threatId`.
- ❌ `enum`, `namespace`, constructor parameter properties (`erasableSyntaxOnly` will reject them).
- ❌ Bare `console.log`, silent `catch {}`, or `try/catch` in the sim.
- ❌ Static import of `src/debug/**` or `src/bots/**` from client code (ships debug to stores).
- **Edge cases to always handle:** multiple simultaneous threats; papa already gliding toward another threat (new command still judged on its own timestamp, choreography chains); gag on threat B during glide to A plays in parallel; press timestamp older than `timeMap` history (clamp + `warn`); Firmness released before `holdUntilMs` = gag; wrong response type inside the window = gag; perfect slow-mo starts at judgement (glide plays in slow-mo); system-spawned threats (e.g. `papa_microsleep` from `fatigue.ts` above 70 fatigue) follow the same FSM and judgement.
- **When the GDD and architecture disagree:** game rules follow the GDD (v2.1), technical mechanics follow the architecture; ADR-010 (single 400 ms threshold) and ADR-002 (windows in sim time) are already reflected in GDD v2.1. If something is still ambiguous, ask Geo rather than guessing.
- **Golden rule:** the AI writes and tests; Geo tunes the feel. Anything Geo might want to tweak must be data, and every feature must stay playable end-to-end without a human (bots, headless CLI, autoplay).
