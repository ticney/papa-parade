## Critical Implementation Rules

### Engine-Specific Rules

**Simulation core (`src/sim`) — pure, deterministic, headless**
- Fixed step `STEP_MS = 10` of *sim time*; client loop = rAF + accumulator, max `MAX_STEPS_PER_FRAME = 10`, render interpolated with `alpha`.
- `timeScale` (slow-mo ×0.3 perfect, ×0.2 last threat, ×0.8 instinct) changes how many steps run per real second — never the step size. Every step is always 10 ms of `simTime`. Effective scale = min(base scale set by `SetTimeScale`, every active slow-mo); slow-mos are `World.time.slowMos`, one per source, see `src/sim/time/slowMo.ts`.
- Hitstop is a *real-time freeze*: sim sets `freezeRealMs`, client loop consumes it (no steps, render keeps going). Sim never reads real time; in headless, freeze is a no-op.
- Pause = loop stops stepping; resume after a 3 s real-time countdown. Never "pause" by setting `timeScale = 0`.
- `World` is plain serializable data: no classes, functions, Maps with object keys, or Three.js refs. Systems are `(world, ctx) => void` run in the fixed order in `step.ts` (commands → time → sequence → director → disruptions → baby → threats → chores → papa → settleChores → physics → gauges → instinct → scoring → flow). Systems never call each other; they read/write `World` and push `SimEvent`s to `ctx.events`. `runGauges` (stories 1.19, 3.1, 4.3) is the fatigue, then the noise, then the need, then the catastrophe (story 3.16, below), then the debug `SetNoise`, `SetBladder` and `SetCatastrophe` (the commanded value is the step's final one). A level opts into fatigue with `gauges.fatigue.microSleepDef`, into noise with `gauges.noise: {}` and into the need with `gauges.need: {}`; `addFatigue` is the only writer of `world.gauges.fatigue`, `addNoise` (with `decayNoise`, the silent 1 %/s fall) of `world.gauges.noisePct`, and `addBladder` of `world.gauges.bladderPct`.
- **The noise gauge (story 3.1).** `feel.noise` (`decayPerSec`, `alertPct`, one row per source in `sources`) and `gauges.noise: {}` in the level. `runNoise`, called by `runGauges` after `runFatigue`, is the one gauge built on events (epics 3.1 AC 3): it decays the value silently (`decayNoise`, no event), then reads **this step's** `PropImpact`, `PhoneRinging` and `BabyCrying` (scanned from the end of `ctx.events` while `t === world.time.sim`, like `isBabyFoundNow`, because `ctx.events` is drained by the caller, not per step) and never the phone's, the props' or the baby's state. Every source goes through `addNoise`, which clamps silently to `[0, 100]` and emits `NoiseChanged{source, noisePct, deltaPct}` only for a real change. A gag whose def has `gagNoisePct` adds it (source `gag`, called by `gagThreat`, story 9.5; `NoiseSource` is the feel's sources plus `debug` and `gag`). So does a missed chore beat whose def has `missNoisePct` (story 9.6: `runNoise` reads `ChoreBeatJudged{fail}` and the def in `ctx.level.chores[choreIndex]`, source `gag`, nothing in a level with no noise gauge). A prop that made noise is silent for `samePropCooldownMs` (`world.gauges.propNoiseAt`), so a bounce is one fall. The baby cries through `startBabyCry` (`sim/baby/cry.ts`, called by `gagThreat` when the def has `gagCryMs`) and `runBabyCry` emits one `BabyCrying` per `BABY_CRYING_EVERY_MS`. The client alerts on an upward crossing of `alertPct` read from `NoiseChanged` (`isNoiseAlertCrossing`): the client event `NoiseAlertReached` (haptic cue `noise_alert`) and the `pp-hud-gauge-alert` shake. At 100 % in a level with noise, Mama wakes (story 3.2, below). The snore (story 4.4) is a source: `runNoise` reads `PapaSnored` (a sequence's failure) and adds `sources.snore.noisePct`.
- **Mama's note (story 1.22, five rows since 3.12).** `World.score` holds what feeds it: `perfects` (+1 in `resolveThreat` on a `perfect`, the micro-sleep's included), `successes` (+1 beside it on a `success`, story 3.12), `nearMisses` (+1 beside the one `NearMiss`, in the stairs gag, and +1 per `NearMissFrozen` at the end of the level, story 3.6) and `calmMs` (the sim time the baby was calm, in whole steps): counters are written at their source (`resolveThreat`; the chore beats of `choreSystem.ts` write `perfects` / `successes` too, story 9.4), no system reads `ctx.events`. `calmMs` is written by the calm tracker (`sim/scoring/calmTracker.ts`, `tickCalm`), called by `runScoring` after `runGauges`: a step is calm while `gauges.noisePct` is strictly below `feel.mama.calmNoiseThresholdPct`, or, in a level without a noise gauge, while the baby is not crying; the step that ends the level counts, the frozen world adds nothing. In the step that ends the level, `runFlow` calls the helper `scoreLevel` right after `LevelEnded`: `computeMamaScore` (pure, `sim/scoring/`) reads the counters, `calmMs` over the time played (`world.time.sim` of that step), `world.mess` (props on the floor, broken props, `mischiefs`; the broken props of the Soupir are the physics' recount `world.mess.propsBroken` **plus the plates the chores broke**, Σ `beatsMissed` × the def's `missBrokenObjects`, story 9.6: `scoreLevel` adds them, `world.mess` itself is not written by a chore), `homePoints` (the Σ of the done house chores' points, story 9.4) and `feel.mama` (slopes, maxima, `starThresholdPoints`), clamps and rounds each of the five rows (Rire, which also counts the parts of Papa's look shown at the end, `lookParts × feel.mama.laughPerLookPartPoints`, story 3.7; Calme du bébé, Maison, Soupir, Hurlement; whole numbers, the Maison row to the nearest half since story 9.4), writes `world.score.final` and emits `LevelScored` (same `t`). The world is frozen from then on, so the note matches the room left on screen. `MamaSchema` makes the maxima (whole numbers) add up to 100 and `baseScorePoints` equal sigh max + scream max, so the five rows (the last two read as « Sans soupir », « Sans hurlement ») always add up to the score. Maison is filled by the house chores (7.5 per chore, 15 at most: 9.4 shipped the cooking, 9.6 the dishwasher). With no chore done the best note is 85 (35 + 20 + 0 + 30), under the 5★ threshold of 90: 4★ was the top (Geo's choice, review of 3.12). With both chores done (since 9.6) the best note is 100 (35 + 20 + 15 + 30). With the cooking alone it is 92.5, so 5★ is reachable from 9.4 on by a flawless game (Geo kept the thresholds in the review of 9.4; the means stay 4★: `perfect` on `salon_kitchen` made 87.2 with the cooking alone, and makes 94.7 with both chores, 30 seeds, story 9.6). `runScoring` is the calm tracker and the combo's carry tick (story 4.2, below). A journal exported before v2.3 has the four-row score (`LegacyScoreSchema`): it parses, and is never replayed with the new note. The client stops the loop on `LevelScored` (`levelSession.finish()`, the sim stays readable) and enters `result`; the Result sequence's timings are `feedback.json › resultScreen` (real ms; `inputGraceMs` makes the screen ignore a press that starts just after it, or its buttons, come up), its stars play the client events `ResultStarStamped` / `ResultLastStarStamped`, whose cues have a `uiElement` visual (the stamped star is the visual twin).
- **Papa's look and Mama's reactions (story 3.7, FR35, FR37, UX-DR13; snapshot version 34).** **The look is world state:** `world.papa.look` (`PapaLook`: `hasMashOnCheek`, `hasHairOnEnd`, `isShirtInsideOut`, all `false` at the start; the type lives in `sim/world.ts` and is exported from `@sim`), written only by `changePapaLook` (`sim/papa/look.ts`), which `gagThreat` calls after `applyGag` / `applyAfterEffect` when the def has a `lookChange` (`PAPA_LOOK_PARTS = mashOnCheek | hairOnEnd | shirtInsideOut`, declared in `content/schemas/threat.ts`): the def says it, not the gag id, so it applies in a hidden zone, a sequence and the final rush, draws nothing from `world.rng`, and a part already shown does nothing (one `PapaLookChanged{t, part, threatId}` per part per level; nothing ever clears one, and it is not an after-effect). Live data: `outlet` → `hairOnEnd`, `coffee_table_object` → `mashOnCheek`, `papa_microsleep` → `shirtInsideOut`; any other def has none (not even the pot's boiled-over purée). `ScoreTally.lookParts` (`lookPartsShown(world.papa.look)`, read by `scoreLevel`) feeds the Rire with `feel.mama.laughPerLookPartPoints` (5), still held to `laughMaxPoints`; the sim report has `lookParts` per run and `lookParts` / `meanLookPartsPerRun` over the runs. The snapshot refuses a `papa.look` that is not an object of exactly the three booleans (`world.papa.look`). **On screen:** `levelScene.update` copies `world.papa.look` into a scratch object every frame (nothing allocated) and `applyLook` draws the merge of the world's and the debug overlay's, through the one `papa.setLook(` of the file; `__PP__.setPapaLook` now sets the overlay (cleared by `resetFx`, never hiding the world's parts) and `fx().papaLook` is the look the view draws. The photo draws the same world, so the look is in the picture; `papaView.applyHair` takes `max(gag, look)`, so the hair stays up after the electrified time. **The reactions are a pure reading, not world state:** `mamaReactionsOf(score, reason)` (`sim/scoring/mamaReactions.ts`, exported from `@sim`) returns `{reaction, points}[]` over `MAMA_REACTIONS = ['laugh', 'sigh', 'scream']` (`shared/constants.ts`) in that order: a reaction plays when its component has points; the Scream also plays whenever the level ended early (`mamaWoke`, `mamaHome`) even with no near miss; with nothing at all she still laughs, worth 0. Calme and Maison have no reaction. No event, no draw, no snapshot field (story 3.8's line choice is a pure function too, with a local seeded draw, still off `world.rng`). **The Result screen** has a `mama` phase (`ResultPhase = photo | mama | rows | stars | replay | actions`; the `replay` phase is story 3.9's) between the photo's hold and the rows: `createResultScreen` takes `reactions` (boot: `resultMamaLines`, the lines `chooseMamaLines` picked for `mamaReactionsOf(score, endReason)` at `LevelScored`, story 3.8) and `onMamaReaction`, which boot turns into the client events `ResultMamaLaughed` / `ResultMamaSighed` / `ResultMamaScreamed` (cues `mama_laugh|sigh|scream` on the `voice` bus, clips `mama_*` of the new `mama` category of `CLIP_PATTERN`, a `uiElement` visual and a `light` / `light` / `medium` haptic). Each reaction shows its line in the `mum-bubble` (`role="status"`, `pp-mum-bubble--laugh|sigh|scream`, ink text on paper, the accent is a bar), a `+N` / `−N` chip (none at 0) and a provisional 2D Mama (inline SVG built with `svgElement`, tokens `mama-skin` and `mama-dress`, one figure per reaction, a bounce / slump / shake on `transform`, at most 3 Hz, none under reduced motion) for `feedback.json › resultScreen.reactionHoldMs` (1 200); the header of a woken or home-early Mama and its door-slam cue still come with the rows. She stands in `pp-result-left` on the polaroid's top-right corner, or alone in the column with no photo. A press (past `inputGraceMs`) in `photo` or `mama` skips them all (none plays) and leaves her at rest on the last reaction, like a rebuilt screen. The five fixed lines 3.7 shipped (`result.mama_line_*` in `strings.fr.json`) moved to `content/mama-lines.json` in story 3.8 (see « Mama's memory (story 3.8) » below). Mama's 3D model stays in E7.
- **Mama's memory (story 3.8, FR36, UX-DR13; no snapshot, journal, golden or `createSim` change).** **Tags are gag ids:** the memory's tag is the sim's `GagId`, taken from the `gag` of every `GagTriggered`, `NearMiss` and `NearMissFrozen` of the level (`mamaTagOf(event)`, `sim/scoring/mamaMemory.ts`, exported from `@sim`); there is no `tags` field on `ThreatDef` (it would move every `levelHash`), and the GDD's « étagère » is the stairs (`stairs_slide`). **They are counted per game, at `LevelScored`:** `boot.ts` keeps `runMamaTags` (a `Set`, cleared in `onLevelStarted` and on `AppStateChanged → loading`, fed from `onSimEvent`), and at `LevelScored`, in this order and before `appFsm.enter('result')`: `chooseMamaLines(…)` with `saveStore.current().mamaMemory` (the games **before** this one), then `saveStore.recordMamaMemory(addRunToMamaMemory(before, runMamaTags))` (fire-and-forget like `setSettings`, `+1` per distinct tag), then the Result screen reads `resultMamaLines`. A rebuilt Result screen (portrait overlay) reuses `resultMamaLines` and never counts again; a level left before its end has no `LevelScored` and counts nothing. **`?autoplay` neither reads nor writes the memory:** it chooses from an empty one and records nothing (`isAutoplaying`, as `sendDifficultyTier`). **The choice is a pure `@sim` function the client calls** (D1: not world state, not a `createSim` option): `chooseMamaLines({ reactions, endReason, runTags, memory, lines, seed })` returns, per reaction in order, `MamaReactionLine = MamaReaction & { lineId, text }`. A line is a candidate when its `reaction` matches and its `when` holds (`tag` in this game and `memory.counts[tag] ≥ minPrevious`; `endReason` equal); the most specific wins (more conditions among `tag` / `endReason`, then the larger `minPrevious`); equals are broken by a **local** `createRng((seed ^ MAMA_LINES_SEED_SALT) >>> 0)` (one stream per call, in reaction order, drawn only on a tie; never `world.rng`); with no specific candidate the reaction's generic lines (no `when`) are the candidates, and `content:check` guarantees one per reaction. **The content:** `content/mama-lines.json` (`MamaLinesSchema`, `ContentBundle.mamaLines`, client-only and outside every hash; registered as `MAMA_LINES_FILE`) holds `{ id, reaction, text, when?: { tag?, minPrevious?, endReason? } }`; `text` reuses `StringValueSchema` (no failure word, tutoiement), is at most 60 characters and has an even number of `*` (`*word*` is emphasis, shown as `<em>` by `client/ui/emphasis.ts`, **never `innerHTML`**; the bubble stays the same live region and its accessible text has no asterisk). `checkMamaLineReferences` (`tools/content/contentChecks.ts`) refuses a `when.tag` that is not in `GAG_IDS`, a `when.endReason` not in `LEVEL_END_REASONS`, a duplicate id and a reaction with no generic line. The lines are placeholders Geo rewrites; the five `result.mama_line_*` strings are gone. **The save is v4:** `mamaMemory: { counts: { [gagId]: games } }` (`MamaMemorySchema`, strict, counts ≥ 1); `SAVE_MIGRATIONS` `{ from: 3 }` adds `{ counts: {} }` and keeps the calibration and the settings. **The test handle:** `__PP__.mamaLines()` returns the lines of the current Result (`null` before the first `LevelScored` and after loading); e2e specs assert the bubble against it and pin known lines by id and text (`tests/e2e/mamaMemory.spec.ts`), and `tests/bots/mamaMemoryLive.test.ts` proves it headless on the live `salon_crawler`.
- **Highlight replay (story 3.9, FR38, UX-DR13, UX-DR15; no sim, snapshot, journal, golden or `createSim` change).** **The rules are client data in `content/feedback.json › replay`** (`ReplaySchema`, `ReplaySettings`; not `feel.json`, which is hashed: a new key there moves every golden and makes every exported journal unreplayable): `preRollMs` 2000, `postRollMs` 1500, `minSpacingMs` 5000, `momentCount` 3, `points { perfect 3, messyGag 2, nearMiss 2, waveLast 1 }`, `messyGagMinProps` 2, `messWindowMs` 1000, `camera { frameWidthM, frameDepthM }` (about 5 × 3.4 m, `[ASSUMPTION]`), real-ms `delayMs` 400 and `endHoldMs` 300. **The snapshots are the client's** (`SNAPSHOT_EVERY_MS`, 5 s): `levelSession` takes one in the loop's `beforeStep`, before the autoplay bot, at `world.time.sim === 0` and at each multiple of 5 s (`endOfLevel/snapshotLog.ts`: `take`, `latestAtOrBefore`, `clear`; never two at one time; cleared by `start()` and `releaseSim`), and exposes `levelSession.replaySource` (`ReplaySource`: the **exact bundle the live sim was created with** (`simFeel`, not `ActiveLevel.feel`: the tweaks are in the journal as `SetFeel`, and a restore refuses another `feelHash`), geometry, level, seed, `journal()`, the log, `endedAt`), readable during `result`. **The moments** (`endOfLevel/highlights.ts`, pure: `createHighlightRecorder()` with `onSimEvent`, `clear`, `pick(endReason, rules)`): a candidate is **one threat at one sim time**, so its events add up; `perfect` is a threat's `ResponseResolved{perfect}` (never a chore beat or a rhythm beat), `messyGag` is a `GagTriggered` followed within `messWindowMs` by at least `messyGagMinProps` **distinct** props hit (`PropImpact.propId` and `otherPropId`) or broken, `nearMiss` is a `NearMiss` (**`NearMissFrozen` scores nothing**: it is the level's end), `waveLast` is the last response of each wave, found in `pick`. `pick` sorts by points, then the **later** `t`, then the lower `threatId`, takes greedily at `minSpacingMs`, and returns chronologically; on **`mamaHome`** the candidates are the game's `GagTriggered` only, latest first (the cascade; the last gag is always in; `mamaWoke` keeps the normal pick). **The moment sim** (`momentSim.ts`: `createMomentSim(source, highlight, rules)` → `Result<MomentSim, AppError>`; `momentWindowOf`): `createSim` with the source, `restore(latestAtOrBefore(fromMs), journal.entries.filter(applyAt > snapshotTime))` (never `enqueue`), a silent fast-forward to `fromMs`; a refusal is an `err` with a `warn` and the sim disposed. **The player** (`highlightReplay.ts`, `createHighlightReplay(deps)`: `play`, `skipMoment`, `skipAll`, `cancel`, `isPlaying`): one moment sim at a time, run by its own `createGameLoop` with a fresh `timeMap` and pause controller at the level's `?speed` (the perfect's slow-mo and the hitstops play as in the game; **the live `timeMap` and loop are never touched, and the live sim is never restored**), stopped at the first step that reaches `untilMs`, held `endHoldMs` on the last frame, drawn through `replayCamera` (`fitFollowCamera` on the focus: the threat's anchor, else Papa) after `level.resetFx()`; **every moment sim is disposed once** (end, skip, cancel, failed build, a build that arrives after a skip). Each event goes to `playSimEventFeedback` and `level.fx.playCharacterEvent`, **except `LevelEnded`, `LevelScored` and `NearMissFrozen`**. **A second `createFeedbackDirector`** (boot's `replayDirector`: same table and audio, no haptics, no flash, `stamp` returns false, `getScene` only while a replay plays, no `onVisual` / `onCue`) so the live director's `cueLog()`, `data-pp-stamp-count` and `data-pp-flash-count` and its `appFsm.state === 'level'` scene gate stay as they were; the replay's events never reach boot's `onSimEvent`, the recorder, the mirrors or the save. After the last moment, a skip or a cancel, `resetFx()` then the frozen live world is drawn through the game camera, so the actions sit on the end of the game. **The Result screen** gets the `replay` phase (`ResultPhase = photo | mama | rows | stars | replay | actions`): `ResultScreenOptions.replay` (`ResultReplay`: `momentCount`, `delayMs`, `play(onDone)`, `skipMoment`, `skipAll`, `cancel`, or `null`; boot's `replayOfResult()`) replaces the star chain's three `later(showActions, …)` with `later(startReplay, delayMs)` when there are moments; under `pp-result-screen--replaying` the scrim, panel, polaroid and Mama are `visibility: hidden` and the `pp-replay-banner pp-type-epic pp-stroked` (« REPLAY », `stamp.replay`, a 240 ms scale-in, none under reduced motion) shows. **The first press during the replay skips a moment, the second the rest** (a press that ends it goes to the buttons at once; with one moment left one press ends it; Enter, Space, the tap and the pad's A, with the same grace and `heldSkipKey` rules); **a press before the replay (`photo` to `stars`) skips it too** and `replay.play` is never called; `dispose()` cancels. **Boot:** `highlightRecorder` (cleared in `onLevelStarted` and on `AppStateChanged → loading`, where it also cancels a replay still playing), `resultHighlights = highlightRecorder.pick(resultEndReason, feedbackTable.replay)` at `LevelScored` (a rebuilt screen reuses it), the mirror `data-pp-replay-moment` (the index playing, removed otherwise and on loading) and `__PP__.highlights()` (`null` before the first `LevelScored` and after loading, `[]` for a game with none; wired like `mamaLines()`). **The e2e specs** that wait for `actions` go through `passReplayToActions` (`tests/e2e/support/game.ts`: Space once or twice while `replay` shows, never in `actions`), so they keep their 15 s budget; `tests/e2e/replay.spec.ts` tests the phases, and `tests/bots/highlightReplayLive.test.ts` proves it headless (`createSim` on the live `salon_kitchen` and `salon_crawler`, `human` and `perfect`, seeds 1 to 3, and the `idle` bot's `mamaHome` games: every moment replays the live events, compared with `hashJson` and never `JSON.stringify`, since `cloneJson` sorts keys).
- **Busy Papa (stories 1.19, 1.20, 4.3, 3.15).** While Papa is `drink`, `asleep`, `onPhone`, `onToilet`, `electrified`, `carryingUp` or `pickingUp` (`isPapaBusy`, a `Record<PapaState, boolean>`: a new state must say whether it is busy; `chore`, story 9.4, is the one state that is **not** busy), `runPapa` ignores `TapFloor`, `TapObject`, `Move`, `MoveStop` and `PressThreat`, `runThreats` refuses a `PressThreat` (story 2.12: it emits `TapRefused{t, threatId}` once per command on a `telegraph` / `windowOpen` threat, and changes nothing else), and the client sends a move, floor, object or baby command never, but **a press on a threat it does send** (`inputMapper`: a tap that hits a threat, or the keyboard / gamepad action with one up, records the finger so that its `Release` follows; no tap-marker, no `onThreatPressed`), because the sim decides: refused, or judged when that step already has Papa free (`now >= busyUntil`); `Release` still passes. A threat whose def has `target: "papa"` (the micro-sleep, and the cry in the arms of story 4.1) is spawned by the sim, stands on Papa, never counts against a wave's `maxSimultaneous`, is never drawn by the director or locked on by the baby, and a press on it does not glide Papa. A threat with `target: "station"` (the pot on the stove, story 9.5) is spawned by the chore system on its station's anchor and is likewise outside the waves, the director's draws and the baby's locks, but a press on it glides Papa as for an anchor threat, and an early press on it is always `guard`.
- **The catastrophe gauge (story 3.16, FR73, GDD mechanic 14; snapshot version 31).** `world.gauges.catastrophePct` (0-100, in every level, no level flag), written only by `addCatastrophe` and `decayCatastrophe` (`sim/gauges/catastrophe.ts`; `runCatastrophe` is called by `runGauges`, not a system of its own). `feel.catastrophe`: `risePct {nearMiss, mess, microSleep, choreBeat}`, `decayPerSec`, `perfectDropPct`, `alertPct` (70) and `faceStepsPct` (the frowning and worried thresholds, strictly ascending and below `alertPct`); each `feel.difficulty` tier has a `catastropheMultiplier` (×0.5 / ×1 / ×1.25) that multiplies the rises only, never the perfect drop or the decay. - Sim emits domain events (PascalCase past tense, `type` discriminant, `t` in sim time). Client drains them after each step → `FeedbackDirector` → `content/feedback.json`. - A cue may `layers` extra sounds played right after its `audio` (the baby's laugh on a perfect: one cue = one moment, no ring or haptic of its own per layer). ### Performance Rules

- **Targets:** 60 FPS stable on iPhone 12 / 2021 Android over a full 5-min level; frame ≤ 16.6 ms with sim ≤ 2 ms (up to 10 steps) and render ≤ 10 ms; tap → visual feedback ≤ 50 ms.
- **Zero allocation in hot paths** (rAF loop, `sim.step()`, systems, view `update`): reuse scratch vectors, no closures/arrays/object literals per frame, no `new Mesh`/`new Vector3` outside pools. `drainEvents()` must not allocate a new array per frame either.
- **Input handlers do almost nothing:** `pointerdown` → timestamp, hit-test, enqueue command, trigger highlight. Visual feedback (highlight + glide start) must appear on the next frame — never wait for the next sim step or papa's arrival.
- **Render budgets:** ≤ 100 draw calls (instance identical props, static furniture merged at export), ≤ 100k on-screen triangles, shared materials, one 256 px palette texture or vertex colors, no texture > 1024 px, blob shadows only (no shadow maps), outlines only on characters / active threats / salient props.
- **DPR:** `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`, stepping down 2 → 1.5 → 1 if mean frame time > 18 ms for 2 s.
- **Loading:** whole level (`.glb` + decoded audio) preloaded behind the loading screen; nothing loads during play. Level payload ≤ 3 MB compressed (< 5 s on 4G), except the big room `salon_kitchen`: 5 MB (NFR15, `LEVEL_PAYLOAD_MAX_BYTES_BY_ID`, checked by `dist:check`; an assumption to confirm with Epic 9's real audio); memory ≤ 300 MB JS + GPU.
- **Logging in hot paths:** nothing above `debug`, and guard expensive formatting with `if (log.enabled('debug'))`.
- `renderer.info` is shown in the debug overlay; perf regressions are caught by `tests/e2e/perf.spec.ts` (bot `human`, full level, fails if p95 frame > 20 ms).

**Layers (top-level of `src/`) and allowed imports — enforced by Biome `noRestrictedImports` + `tests/architecture/layerBoundaries.test.ts`:**

| `shared` | std lib only | any other layer |

| `client` | `@sim` (= `src/sim/index.ts` only), `content`, `shared`, `platform` interface, `three` | `debug` (except guarded dynamic `import()` in `boot.ts`), `bots` (except autoplay, dynamic import) |
| `debug` | anything | — nobody imports it statically |
| `tools/` | anything, Node only | — never imported by `src/` |

- Inside layers, organize by game domain (`sim/threats`, `sim/baby`, `client/input`…); follow the tree in the architecture doc ("Arborescence") — don't invent new top-level folders.
- `client/boot.ts` is the single composition root: dependencies are injected explicitly; no singletons or service locators (only exception: `log`).
- Factories are `create<Name>` and the file is named after its main export (`waveDirector.ts` → `createWaveDirector`). Files `camelCase.ts`; folders lowercase, one word if possible.
- Content: filename = id (`content/threats/outlet.json` ↔ `"id": "outlet"`); content ids `lower_snake_case`; JSON keys `camelCase`; cross-cutting files kebab-case (`mama-lines.json`); generated files `*.geometry.json` and `content/_schemas/` — never edit by hand.

**Naming conventions agents get wrong:**
- **Units are mandatory suffixes:** `windowMs`, `glideMaxMs`, `noisePct`, `speedMps`, `fatiguePerSec`. A bare `duration`/`speed` is a review failure.
- **Time instants:** `t` or `…At` = sim time; `…AtReal` = real time (`pressedAtReal`). Use branded `SimMs` / `RealMs` types; never mix them.
- FSM states: camelCase string-literal unions (`'telegraph' | 'windowOpen' | 'resolved'`), never `enum`. Events: PascalCase past tense, subject first (`ThreatTelegraphed`). Commands: PascalCase imperative (`PressThreat`). Booleans: `is`/`has`/`can`. Constants: `UPPER_SNAKE` in `shared/constants.ts` only. Types: PascalCase, no `I` prefix.
- **Blender (`.glb`) nodes:** `COL_<name>` static collider, `NAV_floor` / `NAV_block_<name>`, `ANCHOR_<anchorId>`, `PROP_<kind>_<nn>` (Rapier prop), `ZONE_hidden_|safe_|creak_<name>`; visual meshes unprefixed; lower_snake_case after prefix; meters, Y-up.
- **Audio:** `public/assets/audio/<category>_<name>.m4a`, categories `tel`, `resp`, `gag`, `baby`, `papa`, `env`, `ui`, `music`, `mama` (Mama's voice on the Result screen, story 3.7, on the `voice` bus).

**Errors & logging:**
- I/O (assets, storage, audio unlock, haptics) returns `Result<T, E>` from `@shared/result` (`{ ok: true, value } | { ok: false, error }`) — no library, no throwing.
- Sim invariants use `invariant()` / `clampInvariant(ctx, value, min, max, name)`: throws in dev/test; in prod clamps and emits `InvariantViolated` (client logs `warn`). No `try/catch` in `src/sim`.
- Every fallback emits at least a `warn` — no silent failure. Uncaught errors → global handler → "Oups" screen + level restart.
- Logging: `log.<level>(channel, message, data?)` from `@shared/log`; channels `boot|assets|audio|input|render|feedback|storage|platform|debug|content|sim`. No `console.*` outside `src/shared/log.ts` (lint rule). The sim never logs — it emits events.

### Platform & Build Rules

- **Targets:** browser first (mobile + desktop, playtest by link); iOS/Android via Capacitor in E8. Inputs: touch, keyboard, gamepad — every action must be doable with all three (keyboard/gamepad use auto-targeting of the most urgent threat in the aimed direction, cone `feel.autoTargetConeDeg`).

- **Input capture:** Pointer Events on the canvas (not mouse/touch events), keyboard, Gamepad API polled each frame; always keep `event.timeStamp` (same timebase as `performance.now()`). ### Critical Don't-Miss Rules

- ❌ `Math.random()`, `Date.now()`, `performance.now()`, `setTimeout`, `crypto.randomUUID()` anywhere in `src/sim` (incl. "just for an id" or "just for debug").
- ❌ Importing `three` (even types like `Vector3`) into `src/sim` — use `@shared/math/vec2|vec3`.
- ❌ Reading or mutating `sim.world` from the client to change gameplay — the client only **reads** `world` (readonly) and **writes** via `sim.enqueue(cmd)`. Same for debug cheats and the feel panel (`SetFeel`).
- ❌ Importing from `src/sim/...` deep paths in client/bots — only `@sim`.
- ❌ Hard-coding a GDD value "for now" (window 300, buffer 100, tolerance 60, hitstop 80, glide 600, thresholds…) — put it in `feel.json` even in a prototype.
- ❌ Judging a response when papa *arrives*, or when the command is *processed* by `step()` — judge on the command's `t`.
- ❌ Real-time vs sim-time confusion: calibration in real time, windows in sim time. Slow-mo widens windows on purpose — don't "fix" it.
- ❌ A "do nothing" threat failing because the player tapped the floor or another threat.
- ❌ Adding an `audio` feedback without `visual` + `haptic` (pillar 3; test will fail).
- ❌ Writing `state = 'x'` directly, or counting frames for durations.
- ❌ Non-serializable data in `World` (class instances, closures, `Map`/`Set`, Three/Rapier handles) — breaks snapshots, replay and golden tests.
- ❌ Calling one sim system from another, or reordering `SYSTEMS` without updating golden tests and justifying it.
- ❌ Inventing a threat target in the sim when several are active — the command carries `threatId`.
- ❌ `enum`, `namespace`, constructor parameter properties (`erasableSyntaxOnly` will reject them).
- ❌ Bare `console.log`, silent `catch {}`, or `try/catch` in the sim.
- ❌ Static import of `src/debug/**` or `src/bots/**` from client code (ships debug to stores).
- **Edge cases to always handle:** multiple simultaneous threats; papa already gliding toward another threat (new command still judged on its own timestamp, choreography chains); gag on threat B during glide to A plays in parallel; press timestamp older than `timeMap` history (clamp + `warn`); Firmness released before `holdUntilMs` = gag; wrong response type inside the window = gag; perfect slow-mo starts at judgement (glide plays in slow-mo); system-spawned threats (e.g. `papa_microsleep` from `fatigue.ts` above 70 fatigue) follow the same FSM and judgement.
- **When the GDD and architecture disagree:** game rules follow the GDD (v2.1), technical mechanics follow the architecture; ADR-010 (single 400 ms threshold) and ADR-002 (windows in sim time) are already reflected in GDD v2.1. If something is still ambiguous, ask Geo rather than guessing.
- **Golden rule:** the AI writes and tests; Geo tunes the feel. Anything Geo might want to tweak must be data, and every feature must stay playable end-to-end without a human (bots, headless CLI, autoplay).
