### Framing
- **[stakes]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Indie commercial (store release planned, E8). Spines written to production depth, not jam depth.
- **[language]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Spines in English (config `document_output_language`), even though GDD/architecture use French by exception; on-screen strings quoted in French verbatim. Game ships **French-only at v1**, i18n-ready: all UI strings in `content/strings.fr.json` (verbs and Mama lines already live in content JSON). English is the first post-v1 locale. Layouts tolerate +30% string length; nothing truncates.
- **[monetization]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Business model still open (GDD). v1 UX reserves **no shop, ad or IAP surface**. Guard rails for later: never interrupt a level; never place anything between the photo and Mama's reaction; any ad or unlock lives on the Fridge between levels only.

### Visual identity (DESIGN.md)
- **[brand]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Two registers mirror the GDD's epic/mundane contrast. **Domestic** (menus, fridge, results, settings): warm, round, chunky, sticker-and-crayon, like a family kitchen. **Epic** (perfect-response stamps, REPLAY banner, final celebration, special-sequence framings): action-movie trailer parody, condensed caps and hard contrast. Domestic is the default; Epic is a short burst and never owns a whole screen.
- **[diegetic-menus]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The home screen is **the family Fridge**. The 5 ages are polaroids held by magnets; stars are sticker magnets; a locked age is an empty slot with a crayon "?". The result screen centres on the **frozen photo as a polaroid**, with Mama's line as a speech bubble. Settings, pause and errors stay plain (non-diegetic) panels for usability.
- **[colors]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Tokens (hex):
  - Surfaces: `cream` #FFF4E3 (menu background), `paper` #FFFFFF (cards, polaroids), `fridge` #E9EEF2 (fridge door), `ink` #2A2140 (text, outlines, hard shadows), `ink-soft` #6B6283 (secondary text), `scrim` rgba(42,33,64,0.62) (pause dim).
  - Brand: `primary` #6D3FE0 (grape; primary buttons, focus ring, brand accents), `primary-press` #5329C2, `on-primary` #FFFFFF.
  - Reserved gameplay signals (only used to mean these things, anywhere in the game): `parry` #1F6BFF (blue + circle), `dodge` #F0353D (red + triangle), `alert` #FFC21A (telegraph halos, "…" silence bubble, auto-target highlight; only during play), `smell` #7CC243 (green trail, diegetic only).
  - Gauges: `gauge-calm` #2ECC9A, `gauge-warn` #FF8A1F (≥70%), track `gauge-track` #2A214033 (ink at 20%).
  - Mama: `mama-laugh` #2ECC9A, `mama-sigh` #8A80A8, `mama-scream` #FF8A1F. Stars: `star` #FFB800 on cream with ink outline (results only; no alert conflict since alert is play-only).
  - Rule: brand grape never appears on a threat. Blue, red and alert yellow never decorate a menu.
- **[typography]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Three families, all SIL OFL, self-hosted woff2 (no network in Capacitor, offline web): **Baloo 2** (display, verbs, headings), **Nunito** (body, labels, numbers with tabular-nums), **Anton** (Epic register only). Roles: `display` Baloo 2 800 56px/1.0; `heading` Baloo 2 700 28px/1.1; `verb` Baloo 2 800 40px/1.0 uppercase (32px compact; floor 28px); `epic` Anton 400 64px/0.95 uppercase, +0.04em; `body` Nunito 700 18px/1.35; `label` Nunito 800 16px/1.2; `numeric` Nunito 900 22px tabular-nums. **16px is the absolute text floor** (the tokens.css minimum from story 1.21).
- **[shape]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Toy-like rounded corners echoing the round low-poly characters: `sm` 8px, `md` 14px, `lg` 22px, `xl` 32px, `full` 9999px. Buttons are `full` pills; panels are `lg`; polaroids are `sm` with a slight random tilt of ±3°.
- **[elevation]** `[ASSUMPTION]` No soft shadows. **Sticker depth**: 3px `ink` outline + a hard offset shadow `0 4px 0 ink`, matching the 3D inverted-hull outlines. Pressed = shadow collapses to 0 and the element translates 4px down. No `backdrop-filter` blur (mobile performance); the pause overlay dims with `scrim`.
- **[spacing]** `[ASSUMPTION]` 4px base: 1=4, 2=8, 3=12, 4=16, 5=24, 6=32, 7=48. Screen margin = `max(16px, env(safe-area-inset-*))`. HUD inset = `max(12px, safe-area)`.
- **[legibility-over-3D]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Every world-anchored text (verbs, combo) is white fill + 5px `ink` stroke (`paint-order: stroke`) + hard shadow, so it reads over any room colour. HUD chips sit on `paper` pills with an ink outline, never bare over the scene.
- **[dark-mode]** `[ASSUMPTION]` No dark theme; the game owns its look and ignores the OS scheme. Night play is a real case (journey 1), so menus use `cream`, never pure white backgrounds, and the start screen is mostly the dim nursery scene.

### Experience (EXPERIENCE.md)
- **[ia]** `[ASSUMPTION]` App flow on top of the locked appFsm (boot → calibration → menu → level → result):
  - **Start** ("Touche pour commencer"; title art plus nursery scene) → first gesture unlocks audio.
  - First launch → **Calibration** (audio phase, then visual phase) → straight into **Âge 1** with no menu ("immediate pick-up").
  - Returning launch → **Fridge** (home).
  - **Fridge** → age polaroid → Loading → Level. A magnet icon top-right opens **Réglages** (Credits live inside Réglages).
  - **Level** → Pause overlay (Reprendre · Recommencer · Réglages · Quitter vers le frigo).
  - **End of level**: door → final rush → photo → Mama → **Result**, with the actions Rejouer · Âge suivant · Frigo.
  - Overlays reachable from anywhere: "Tourne ton téléphone" (portrait), "Oups" (errors).
  - Max depth 2 from the Fridge. Overlays never stack: Réglages opened from Pause replaces Pause and returns to it.
- **[hud]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Within the locked zones (story 1.4):
  - **Top-left gauges**: at most 3 at once (Fatigue, Bruit in Sieste only, Envie). Icon chip + 10-segment bar; ordered Bruit, Fatigue, Envie.
  - **Top-centre**: timer as a pill with an icon per variant (sleeping Mama for Sieste, door for Retour). During a special sequence, its **sequence bar** (boss-bar parody: diaper health, calm) slides in directly under the timer, and the timer shrinks to compact.
  - **Top-right**: pause button.
  - **World-anchored**: combo badge above Papa (visible from x2); breath ring around Papa during stealth; attention meter above the toddler.
  - **Mama score is never shown during play.** It is revealed only at the end (the payoff), while Papa's degrading look is the diegetic hint.
  - The whole HUD band stays ≤ 12% of the room height on a 375px-tall phone.
- **[hud-fade]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Gauges under 50% idle at 70% opacity and go to full opacity when changing or ≥ 70%. During photo, Mama reactions and replay, the HUD hides entirely. During slow-mo it stays.
- **[voice]** `[ASSUMPTION]` French, **tutoiement**, short.
  - Domestic register is warm and wry: "Touche pour commencer", "Le frigo".
  - Epic register is trailer-parody caps: "PARADE PARFAITE", "REPLAY".
  - Verbs are imperative, one word where possible, uppercase with "!".
  - Failure is never named as failure: no "échec", "perdu", "game over", "mort".
  - Never mock the baby or shame the parent. Mama is exasperated-loving, never cruel.
  - Error copy is light: « Oups ! Quelque chose s'est renversé. » + "Réessayer".
- **[input]** `[ASSUMPTION]`
  - **Keyboard**: bound by `KeyboardEvent.code` (physical position), so ZQSD on AZERTY and WASD on QWERTY both work with no setting; arrows always work. On-screen key glyphs use `navigator.keyboard.getLayoutMap()` when available, else arrows and "Espace".
  - **Gamepad**: glyphs follow the detected family (PlayStation ✕○ / Xbox-style A B default).
  - **Prompts**: touch shows no prompts (the world is the prompt). Keyboard/gamepad shows one glyph chip beside the auto-targeted threat's verb.
  - **Remapping** is out of v1 scope (revisit for Steam).
  - **Menus** are fully navigable by keyboard and gamepad, with a visible focus ring. Esc / B / Android back = back, or pause in play.
- **[hold-feedback]** `[ASSUMPTION]` On press over a threat, a ring fills around the threat icon over 400ms (`holdMinMs`). Releasing before the ring closes = Parade; ring closes = Fermeté (it snaps with a medium haptic). The same ring is used for sniff (1s on baby) and the breath hold.
- **[juice-ui]** `[ASSUMPTION]`
  - Buttons react on `pointerdown` (sticker press) and act on `pointerup` inside.
  - Panels pop in with overshoot (≤ 220ms).
  - Stars stamp in one by one (sound + light haptic each, heavy on the last).
  - Score rows count up.
  - A new unlock on the Fridge drops its polaroid with a magnet "thunk".
  - "PARADE PARFAITE" Epic stamp on perfect responses, ≤ 900ms, in the Epic register.
- **[reduced-motion]** `[ASSUMPTION]` A "Réduire les animations" setting:
  - Removed: screen shake, perfect-camera rotation; zoom capped at 50%.
  - Reduced: particle counts by 60%; UI overshoot becomes a plain fade; the full-screen flash becomes a border glow; the "…" bubble pulses slowly (≤ 1Hz) instead of blinking.
  - Unchanged: hitstop and slow-mo (they are gameplay timing).
  - Nothing ever flashes faster than 3Hz.
- **[settings]** `[ASSUMPTION]` v1 Réglages:
  - Difficulté (Assistance / Normal / Papa expérimenté, labelled "appliqué à la prochaine partie").
  - Volumes: Musique, Effets, Voix, Interface (the four audio buses).
  - Vibrations on/off (hidden when `capabilities.haptics=false`).
  - Réduire les animations.
  - Grands verbes (+25% verb size).
  - Recalibrer.
  - Crédits.
  - Changes save instantly (no Save button).
- **[calibration-muted]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The audio phase offers « Pas de son ? Passe au visuel », because the iOS silent switch mutes web audio and night players play muted. Skipping keeps the visual phase only.
- **[interrupt]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Real-life interruption is a first-class case for parent players. Auto-pause on background, gamepad disconnect, or a portrait rotation. Resuming always goes through the 3s countdown (large Baloo digits plus tick sound, haptic and pulse). Quitting mid-level asks for confirmation inline, with a second tap on "Quitter vers le frigo" to confirm; no stacked modal.
- **[responsive]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Landscape surfaces:
  - **Compact**: short side < 430px (phones).
  - **Regular**: short side ≥ 430px (tablets, desktop).
  - Desktop adds hover states and mouse focus.
  - The HUD corners never move between surfaces.
- **[perf-ui]** `[ASSUMPTION]` HUD budget ≤ 40 DOM nodes. Gauges animate via `transform: scaleX` only (no layout). CSS2D labels are pooled. No blur filters. HUD updates are batched once per frame.
- **[journeys]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The GDD names none, so three are written from the audience:
  1. **Julien**, 33, first-time dad, 22:40 on the couch, iPhone 12 with sound off because the baby just fell asleep. First launch through his first perfect parry.
  2. **Priya**, an external playtester on a QWERTY laptop with a keyboard, from the playtest link. Mama wakes, she loses a star, laughs, and copies the journal.
  3. **Karim**, 41, returning player with a gamepad on desktop. His real baby cries mid-level; he comes back 10 minutes later, resumes, finishes the final rush with 2 stars, and unlocks the next age on the Fridge.
- **[inspiration]** `[ASSUMPTION]`
  - **Embrace**: Overcooked (warm hand-made menus, chaos-as-comedy), WarioWare (giant verbs), Peggle (celebration), Sekiro (perfect-response audio), Untitled Goose Game (minimal HUD, world-first).
  - **Reject**: text-tutorial popups, game-over or fail screens, HUD covering the room, mid-level ads, tiny corner buttons, guilt-trip copy.
- **[mocks]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Key screens to render: in-level HUD (phone landscape), Fridge (age select), Result (V1), Pause + resume countdown. Start, calibration, loading, settings, Oups and portrait stay spine-only.

### Gap resolution after first distill (facilitator, under delegation)
- **[input-pause]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Fixes a conflict with story 5.1 (B + direction = dodge). **In play**, pause is Start/Options (gamepad), Esc (keyboard), Android back or the on-screen button. B never pauses. **In menus**, B / Esc / Android back = back.
- **[gauge-contrast]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Retunes gauges for ≥ 3:1 non-text contrast (WCAG 1.4.11).
  - Track becomes `paper` #FFFFFF; every segment gets a 2px `ink` outline.
  - `gauge-calm` darkens to #1F9E73; `gauge-warn` darkens to #E06A00.
  - The warning state also pulses its icon chip, so it never relies on hue alone.
  - `mama-laugh` and `mama-scream` follow the new gauge hexes.
- **[open-visuals]** `[ASSUMPTION]`
  - **hold-ring**: 5px stroke; `paper` progress over an ink-40% track; turns solid `paper` with a pop on close (Fermeté). Never threat-coloured.
  - **tap-marker**: `paper` ring with an `ink` outline; scales from 0.6 to 1.2 and fades over 300ms.
  - **instinct-vignette**: `ink` at 35% radial edge darkening. Never red (red = dodge).
  - **attention-meter**: 5 pips above the toddler; same fill rules as hud-gauge.
  - **sequence-bar**: fill depends on what it measures. Opponent "health" (the diaper boss) uses `gauge-warn`; player resources (calme, souffle) use `gauge-calm`.
  - **loading-indicator**: a baby bottle outline (ink) filling with `gauge-calm`. Under reduced motion, a static fill.
  - **calibration-pulse**: the calibration screen uses an `ink` background (dim, night-friendly). Audio beats show a `paper` ring pop; the visual phase uses a `paper` disc flash at 100 BPM (1.67Hz, under the 3Hz limit).
- **[result-actions]** `[ASSUMPTION]`
  - **≥ 2★ with the next age unlocked**: primary "Âge suivant", secondary "Rejouer", icon "Frigo".
  - **< 2★**: primary "Rejouer". "Âge suivant" stays visible but locked, with the hint "2 étoiles pour débloquer".
  - **Adulte (last age)**: "Âge suivant" is replaced by primary "Frigo". The first completion of Adulte with ≥ 1★ plays the credits, then returns to the Fridge.
- **[copy]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Canonical French strings, added to the voice table:
  - **Calibration**: « Tape en rythme avec les bips » / « Tape à chaque flash » / « Pas assez de taps, on recommence ? » / « Pas de son ? Passe au visuel » / « Calé ! ».
  - **Loading** (rotating, flavour only, never instructions): « Préparation du biberon… » / « On range les jouets… » / « On cache les clés de voiture… ».
  - **Pause**: « Pause », « Reprendre », « Recommencer », « Réglages », « Quitter vers le frigo », with the confirmation « Sûr ? Le niveau sera perdu. ». Countdown: « 3 » « 2 » « 1 ».
  - **Gamepad**: « Manette débranchée. Rebranche-la pour reprendre. »
  - **Result**: « Maman s'est réveillée ! », « Nouvel âge débloqué ! », « 2 étoiles pour débloquer », « Rejouer », « Âge suivant », « Frigo », « Copier le journal » → « Journal copié ! ».
  - **Score rows**: « Rires », « État du bébé », « Soupirs », « Hurlements ».
  - **Fridge**: « Nouveau ! ».
  - **Settings**: « Difficulté », « Assistance » (« Fenêtres plus larges, instinct activé »), « Normal » (« Le jeu tel qu'il est pensé »), « Papa expérimenté » (« Fenêtres serrées, aucune aide »), « Appliqué à la prochaine partie », « Musique », « Effets », « Voix », « Interface », « Vibrations », « Réduire les animations », « Grands verbes », « Recalibrer », « Crédits ».
  - **Epic**: « PARADE PARFAITE », « REPLAY ».
- **[derived]** The distill agent's *derived* values (secondary button, settings controls, stamp colours) are accepted as `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- **[open-sweep]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Second sweep of the items the distill left open.
  - **Visual details**:
    - **target-highlight vs telegraph halo**: the halo is a soft pulsing `alert` glow. The target-highlight is a solid 4px `alert` outline plus a small bouncing `alert` chevron (ink outline) above the verb label. They differ by shape, not by colour.
    - **threat-icon size**: 56px diameter in compact, 64px in regular. The touch radius stays in feel.json.
    - **combo-badge type**: new token `typography.combo` = Baloo 2 800 28px/1.0.
    - **Focus ring**: 4px `primary`, 3px offset. Over the 3D scene, a 2px `paper` inner ring is added.
    - **settings-toggle off**: `paper` track with an ink outline, `ink-soft` thumb.
    - **Locked "?"**: drawn in crayon in `ink-soft`.
    - **Polaroid captions**: polaroids show the age name in `label` on the bottom strip.
    - **Compact timer**: the numeric drops to 18px and the pill to 32px tall.
  - **Calibration and loading**:
    - Calibration accepts tap, Espace or A.
    - Calibration in background: the current phase pauses and restarts on return.
    - Loading in background: the load continues, and the level waits for the foreground, then starts through the 3s countdown.
  - **attention-meter**: visible throughout Petite enfance levels, with the same idle-opacity rule as hud-gauge.
  - **Save failure**: silent for the player (defaults restored, ARCH). Calibration re-runs as on a first launch.
  - **Repeated failure**: after 3 consecutive ≤ 1★ results on the same level outside Assistance, the Result screen shows one gentle card, « Envie de souffler ? Essaie le mode Assistance », with a button to Réglages. Never shown in Assistance; dismissable.
  - **Journal**: "Copier le journal" appears in playtest (web) builds only, behind a build flag, and is hidden in store builds. If the clipboard API fails, the button becomes « Télécharger le journal » (a .json download).
  - **Default focus**: first action of a panel; first row in Réglages; "Réessayer" on Oups.
  - **Quit arming**: the button label becomes « Sûr ? Le niveau sera perdu. » with an `ink` fill and `paper` text. It reverts after 3s.
  - **Result sequence order** (GDD macro loop):
    1. Mama's reactions.
    2. Score rows and stars stamp in.
    3. The highlights replay plays (skippable).
    4. The Result actions appear.
  - **Accessibility scope**:
    - Every DOM button carries a French `aria-label`. Menus are screen-reader navigable; gameplay is not, which is stated as a limit.
    - Mama's voice lines are always on screen (mum-bubble), which covers voice captions.
    - The touch scheme is single-finger, so the game is one-hand playable by design.

### Rulings on the key-screen renderer's flags (facilitator, under delegation)
- **[dodge-verb]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The default dodge verb is « ESQUIVE ! ». A threat's content JSON may override it with a more specific verb (FR53 still requires one on every threat).
- **[gauge-layout]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The three top-left gauges sit side by side in **one horizontal `paper` pill**, ordered left to right Bruit · Fatigue · Envie, each an icon plus a 10-segment bar. Stacking is rejected because it breaks the ≤ 12% band, which is about 45px on a 375px phone. Each bar is a single `scaleX` fill under a segment mask (node budget).
- **[score-rows]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The four rows show **points earned out of their maximum** so they add up to the score: « Rires » /40 · « État du bébé » /30 · « Sans soupir » /15 · « Sans hurlement » /15. This replaces the labels « Soupirs » and « Hurlements » from the copy entry.
- **[lost-star]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The lost star (Mama woke early) shows as a dashed `ink-soft` empty star plus a « −1 » chip in `mama-scream`.
- **[button-tertiary]** `[ASSUMPTION]` New component **button-tertiary**: a transparent pill with a 3px `ink` outline, no lift. It is used for "Frigo" on Result and the lowest-priority actions.
- **[quit-armed]** Keeps the logged label « Sûr ? Le niveau sera perdu. » (`ink` fill, `paper` text, reverts after 3s). The renderer's invented strings are rejected.
- **[countdown-type]** `[ASSUMPTION]` New token `typography.countdown`: Baloo 2 800 112px/1.0.
- **[pause-hud]** `[ASSUMPTION]` During Pause and the countdown, the HUD stays visible under `scrim`, dimmed and inert.
- **[star-scope]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The `star` token covers earned-result display on Result and on the Fridge magnets. It never appears during play.
- **[fridge-labels]** `[ASSUMPTION]` A small « Le frigo » header is allowed. Locked slots keep their age name in `ink-soft` to build anticipation. « Nouveau ! » and the magnets use `primary`.
- **[mama-woke-line]** Mama's lines stay content-driven (`mama-lines.json`, most specific line wins, story 3.8). The mock's reuse of « *Encore* l'étagère ?! » is illustrative only.
- **[hud-composite]** HUD state B is a reference composite of two threats in one frame. The caption says so.
- **[hold-ring-contrast]** `[ASSUMPTION]` The hold-ring gets a 2px `ink` outer outline, so its `paper` progress reads over a light room (the progress-to-outline contrast becomes about 15:1).
- **[locked-next / pad-disconnect]** `[ASSUMPTION]` Accepted as distilled: a locked « Âge suivant » has an `ink-soft` label and no shadow, and the gamepad-disconnect line sits on the Pause panel.
- **[promote]** The four key-screen mocks are promoted to `mockups/` (key-hud-level, key-fridge, key-result, key-pause). `.working/` copies stay as the audit trail. Spine-only surfaces: Start, Calibration, Loading, Réglages, Oups, Tourne ton téléphone, Credits, all built from the spine tables alone.
- **[finalize]** Spines finalized 2026-10-26. Both are `status: final`; Pass 1 is strong on all 5 categories (30 components, 48 token references resolve, 4 mockups linked). Reviewer Gate not run (the developer may opt in later via Validate). `doc_standards` skills (`bmad-editorial-review-structure` / `-prose`) are not installed in this environment, so they were skipped. No external handoffs are configured. Every `[ASSUMPTION]` awaits Geo's review.
