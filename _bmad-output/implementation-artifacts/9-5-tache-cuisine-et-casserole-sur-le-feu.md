# Story 9.5: Tâche « cuisine » et casserole sur le feu

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a player,
I want to cook the purée while keeping an eye on the baby,
so that I earn Maison points without letting the pot burn.

**Requirements covered:** FR76 (the kitchen chore: four beats « remuer, écraser, remuer, couper le feu », spaced 1.5 to 3 s, a missed beat is a small gag, « purée qui attache (fumée) »; « une cuisson abandonnée plus de 30 s devient la perturbation casserole sur le feu »), FR30 revised (sprint change proposal 2026-11-03: « casserole sur le feu : conséquence d'une cuisson de la tâche cuisine laissée seule plus de 30 s », Parer : baisser le feu), FR73 (its catastrophe rise: GDD « casserole qui déborde +20 % (bazar) »), FR72 (its séquelle: the mess stays on the floor and on the photo). **Depends on:** 9.4 (house chores, `cooking` on the `stove` of `salon_kitchen`, the `paused` state and its `enteredAt`: done and merged, `main` at `03e7630`), 9.3 (off-screen bubble: the pot is a threat, so it gets one for free), 9.2 (follow camera), 3.16 (catastrophe), 3.15 (séquelles, `mess`), 3.13 (fairness), 3.1 (noise gauge), 2.11 (chrono-ring), 1.19 / 4.1 (threats the sim spawns itself: the micro-sleep, the cry in the arms — the model for the pot).

**Scope:** dress 9.4's `cooking` as a cooking, and give it its consequence.
1. **The cooking's beats have verbs**: each beat of a chore names its verb (`verbKey`), shown at the station while the beat is announced: « REMUE ! », « ÉCRASE ! », « REMUE ! », « COUPE LE FEU ! ».
2. **A missed beat is « la purée attache »**: its own cue, with smoke (the `gag` fx preset's puff particles at the stove).
3. **The pot on the stove** (« casserole sur le feu »): a cooking started then left `paused` for 30 s spawns a threat **on the station** (new threat target `station`), « BAISSE LE FEU ! », parried like any threat (chrono-ring, verb, off-screen bubble, glide, judgement, Rire, combo); missed, it boils over: noise (+10 %), the mess left on the floor until the end and on the photo (`mess` séquelle, Soupir), the catastrophe +20 % (`mess`).

**Not in this story:**
- The dishwasher (9.6): its station kind, its broken plate, its open door as a target of the baby. 9.6 reuses `abandonedThreat` only if it fits (the open dishwasher is a target the baby walks to, so it is probably a wave threat, not a `station` one: 9.6 decides).
- The new mischiefs of the room (9.7); a pot as a wave threat (the pot is never planned by a wave: only an abandoned cooking makes one).
- Moving the `pan` node of `salon_kitchen` (a lid that rattles, steam), Papa's stirring / mashing poses per verb: E7's art (`deferred-work.md`, 9.1: « The E9 moving pieces are static nodes »).
- The `human` bot's off-screen visibility model and the 200-game balance of `salon_kitchen` (9.8).
- Any change to the levels without a cooking (`salon_crawler`, `chambre_night`, the test levels): same journals, same events, same hashes (AC 12).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Each beat has its verb** (AC 1 of the epic). `ChoreDefSchema`'s beat gains a required `verbKey` (`verb.<name>`, a key of `strings.fr.json`'s `verb.` group; `content:check` refuses a missing string with the threats' wording). `content/chores/cooking.json`: beats `verb.stir` (2000 ms), `verb.mash` (2500), `verb.stir` (1500), `verb.turn_off_heat` (3000); `strings.fr.json` gains `verb.stir` « REMUE ! », `verb.mash` « ÉCRASE ! », `verb.turn_off_heat` « COUPE LE FEU ! » and `verb.lower_heat` « BAISSE LE FEU ! ». While a beat is announced (`beatState === 'telegraph'`), the station's marker shows that beat's verb above its icon, in the threats' verb style (`pp-verb` look, the « Grands verbes » scale `--pp-verb-scale`, fitted like a threat's verb); no verb when no beat is announced (held, paused, done). The text is translated once at mount and written only when the beat changes. `npm run content:schemas` regenerates `chore.schema.json`.
2. **A missed beat makes the purée stick** (AC 1 of the epic: « raté : purée qui attache (fumée) »). `cooking.json › feedback.miss` is a new cue `chore_puree_stuck`: an existing clip (no new audio file), a `soundWave` on the `object`, haptic `medium`, and `fx: "gag"`, whose puff particles are the smoke, at the stove (`feedbackDirector.ts` starts a cue's `fx` particles where its world `visual` ring starts, l.199-205, and `anchorOf` already resolves `object`: so the cue needs its `soundWave` on `object`, nothing to change in the director; pin it with a `feedbackDirector.test.ts` case). The +10 % `choreBeat` rise is unchanged (9.4). `chore_beat_missed` stays in `feedback.json` only if something still names it (else remove it; `content:check` reports unused cues only if it already does).
3. **The pot is a threat def on a station.** `THREAT_TARGETS` gains `'station'` (« on a chore's station: only the chore system spawns it »); a `station` def must have `response: "parry"` (schema refine) and may not be planned by a wave (`simLevelOf` invariant and `content:check`, the wording of the `papa` / `baby` rule). `THREAT_ICONS` gains `pot` (the client's `THREAT_ICON_PATHS` draws it: reuse the stove's saucepan path or a pot with steam, placeholder). A new optional threat key `gagNoisePct` (number, > 0, ≤ 100; absent = the gag makes no noise), allowed on any threat like `gagCryMs`. New file `content/threats/pot_on_stove.json`: `target: "station"`, `response: "parry"`, `telegraphDelayMs: 2000`, `windowMs: null`, `gag: "pot_boiled_over"`, `verbKey: "verb.lower_heat"`, `icon: "pot"`, `iconShape: "circle"`, `color: "parry"`, `feedback { telegraph: "tel_pot_on_stove", success: "resp_parry", perfect: "resp_parry_perfect", gag: "gag_pot_boiled_over" }`, `catastrophe: "mess"`, `afterEffect: { "kind": "mess" }`, `cooldownMs: 1000`, `gagNoisePct: 10`. `content:schemas` regenerates `threat.schema.json`.
4. **The cooking names its pot.** `ChoreDefSchema` gains an optional `abandonedThreat: { def, afterMs }` (no default; `def` a threat id whose file has `target: "station"`, `afterMs` a whole positive number of sim ms). `cooking.json`: `"abandonedThreat": { "def": "pot_on_stove", "afterMs": 30000 }`. `loadLevelContent` loads that threat def with the level (after every threat it loads today, so that no index of `salon_kitchen`'s existing threats moves); `content:check` refuses an unknown def, a def whose target is not `station`, and a `station` def named by no chore. `simLevelOf` plans one threat instance per chore that has an `abandonedThreat` (invariants: loaded, target `station`, response `parry`, gag known and paired with its `afterEffect` / `catastrophe`), **appended after every other planned threat**, standing at the station object's anchor point (`anchorId` = the object's anchor), `firstWaveIndex: -1` (in no wave's pool, weight 0 against `maxSimultaneous`); `SimPlannedChore` gains `abandoned: { threatIndex, afterMs } | null`.
5. **Thirty seconds abandoned, the pot starts** (AC 2 of the epic). In a step where a chore with an `abandonedThreat` is `paused`, its pot instance is `idle`, `now ≥ max(chore.enteredAt, pot.enteredAt) + afterMs`, the flow is `playing`, Papa is not busy (`isPapaBusy`), Papa is not gliding to that station, and the pot could be judged before the level ends (`isJudgeableBeforeEnd`), the chore system telegraphs the pot (`telegraphThreat(..., waveIndex -1, postponeMs)`, with `postponeMs` from `fairWindowOpenAt`, D5): `ThreatTelegraphed{kind: "pot_on_stove", anchorId: "stove"}`. An `untouched`, `working` or `done` cooking never makes one. After the pot is over (resolved or gagged, then re-armed after its cooldown, or cancelled), the next one is due `afterMs` after it went `idle` again if the cooking is still `paused` (`pot.enteredAt`), so an abandoned cooking keeps asking for Papa every 30 s or so.
6. **Lowering the heat is a parry.** The pot is a threat like any other: its marker, chrono-ring and verb « BAISSE LE FEU ! » at the stove; the off-screen bubble when the stove is out of frame (9.3, nothing to add); a tap / Space / A press glides Papa to it and is judged on its `t`; `perfect` / `success` count for Rire and the combo, cause the threats' slow-mo / hitstop rules, lower the catastrophe on a perfect. The baby never locks on it or crawls to it (`lockOnThreat` skips `station` as it skips `papa`); taking the baby in the arms does **not** cancel it (`takeBaby` cancels the baby's threats only); `ForceThreat` ignores it (sim-spawned, as `papa` / `baby`); a special sequence's start cancels it like every threat (`ThreatCancelled{sequence}`); the danger tint glows on its anchor (`anchorThreatDefIds` includes `station` defs).
7. **Missed, the pot boils over** (AC 2 of the epic: « bruit, bazar »). ## Dev Notes

### Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **The pot is a threat, not a disruption.** A threat def with the new target `station`, spawned by the chore system, at the station's anchor. | GDD mechanic 16 and the disruptions table: « Parer : baisser le feu ». In the sim a disruption is « not a threat: nobody parries it » (`schemas/disruption.ts` header); a parry with its chrono-ring, verb, window, judgement, gag, séquelle and catastrophe is exactly the threat machinery, and with it the off-screen bubble (9.3), the bots, the fairness and the feedback come for free. The micro-sleep and the cry in the arms already prove a threat the sim spawns itself. **Variance** from the architecture (`content/disruptions/pot.json`, « E6 : dog.json, pot.json ») and from `DISRUPTION_KINDS`' comment (« The E6 dog and pot join the phone here »): say it in the notes and fix that comment. |
| D2 | **A new target `station`**, not `anchor`. | An `anchor` threat is the baby's mischief: the baby locks on it and crawls to it, taking the baby cancels it, a wave can plan it, `ForceThreat` starts it. None fits a pot on the fire. `station` is excluded where `papa` / `baby` are (AC 6), and a wave cannot plan it. |
| D3 | **The rule lives on the chore def** (`abandonedThreat: { def, afterMs }`, optional, no default). | « Une cuisson abandonnée plus de 30 s » is the cooking's rule (GDD 250), so its number is with the cooking's other numbers; the threat def keeps what the threat is. Optional, so 9.6's dishwasher (a target of the baby, not a pot) can do without it. Only chore files change, so no other level's hash moves. |
| D4 | **The timer is `max(chore.enteredAt, pot.enteredAt) + afterMs`, no new world field.** | `paused` and its `enteredAt` were made for this (9.4 D4); `pot.enteredAt` is written by `enterThreatState` when the pot goes back to `idle` (re-armed or cancelled).
