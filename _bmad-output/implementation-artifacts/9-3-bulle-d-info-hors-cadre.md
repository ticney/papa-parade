# Story 9.3: Bulle d'info hors cadre

Status: done

## Story

As a player,
I want to know that a mischief is brewing off screen, and where,
so that I can get there in time.

**Requirements covered:** FR77 (the off-screen bubble; the room is 9.1, the camera 9.2), FR19 (v2.3: in the big room the « … » bubble stays without arrow or direction), UX-DR30, UX-DR26 (the HUD's node budget holds with 3 bubbles). **Depends on:** 9.2 (follow camera, `followRig`, `followCamera.ts`, done), 2.11 (chrono-ring helpers, done), 1.21 (silence bubble, done), 1.17 (auto-target, done), 1.5 (HUD zones), 2.12 (busy Papa's refused press, done).

**Scope:** in a `follow` level only, a screen-anchored bubble at the left, right or top edge for each active threat whose icon is outside the follow frame (at most 3), with the threat's icon, its chrono-ring as the marker draws it and an ink chevron toward it; tapping it is the same `PressThreat` as tapping the threat; keyboard and gamepad targeting shows on it; the silence bubble loses its direction in a follow level; the tests and debug mirrors that pin all of it. **No sim change, no content change, no golden change.**

**Not in this story:**
- The `human` bot's visibility model (« il ne réagit à une menace hors cadre qu'après l'apparition de sa bulle », 9.8). The in-frame test is written pure, in `src/shared`, so that 9.8 can reuse it without `three` (D2).
- New threats in the kitchen (9.5–9.7). Today no planned threat of `salon_kitchen` leaves the frame on a landscape phone (measured, below): the tests make it happen on purpose (a 4:3 viewport, a tighter frame through `setFeel`, `ForceThreat`).
- Chores and their posts (9.4): a post is not a threat; no bubble for it here.
- Any change to the fixed camera levels (`salon_crawler`, `chambre_night`): no bubble there, ever, and their screenshots do not move.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **A bubble for each active threat outside the frame** (AC 1 of the epic). In a `follow` level, a threat in `telegraph` or `windowOpen` whose icon point (the marker entry's `xM, yM, zM`) is outside the follow frame (D2) has an `offscreen-bubble` in `#pp-hud`, from the frame where it is outside, whether it telegraphed outside or the camera left it behind. The bubble disappears the frame the icon is back inside the frame (with the hysteresis of D2, so it never flickers at the edge), or the threat leaves `telegraph | windowOpen` (resolved, gag, cancelled, rearmed to `idle`), or the level stops being live (end, restart, `loadLevel`, unmount). A `hidden` threat (a silence before the baby is found) never has one. A `fixed` level never has one.
2. **Where it sits** (UX-DR30, DESIGN › Layout & Spacing). The bubble's centre is on a U-shaped track (D3): the left edge, the top edge, the right edge, inside `{spacing.hud-inset}`. Top-edge bubbles sit just under the HUD row (`.pp-hud-row`'s bottom + `{spacing.2}`) and never cover a HUD element; side-edge bubbles stay between the HUD row and the bottom third. No part of a bubble (its chevron included) is ever in the bottom third (`y > 2/3` of the viewport height). A threat below the frame shows at the bottom of the nearer side edge, above the bottom third. The bubble's place along the track is where the line from the frame's centre to the threat's projected icon point crosses it (D3).
3. **What it shows** (DESIGN › offscreen-bubble). A `{colors.paper}` round bubble with a 3 px `{colors.ink}` outline, at least `{spacing.touch-target-min}` (44 px); inside, the threat's icon with its shape and colour (circle parry blue, triangle dodge red: the very icon the marker builds, D5), 40 px on a compact surface and 48 px on a regular one; the threat's chrono-ring (only for a response that has one, `hasChronoRing`: never on `wait` or `nothing`), showing at every frame exactly what the marker's ring shows (progress per-mille, lit arc, notch, a held Firmness's drain: D6); an `{colors.ink}` chevron on the bubble's rim pointing at the threat's projected icon point (never a triangle in a threat colour). No animation: it pops in and out, so it needs no reduced-motion rule.
4. **At most 3, stacked without overlap** (AC 4 of the epic). At most 3 bubbles at once; with more than 3 threats outside the frame, the 3 most urgent (earliest `windowOpenAt + responseWindow.windowMs`, ties to the lower id: `urgency.ts`) have one. Bubbles that would touch keep a `{spacing.2}` (8 px) gap between their outer edges (chevrons included), and wrap around a corner of the U when an edge is full (D3): with 3 threats on the same side of the frame, the 3 bubbles never overlap, at 667 × 375, 750 × 340, 844 × 390 and 1024 × 768, on both surfaces. While the silence bubble (and its ring) shows, no off-screen bubble overlaps it either (D8).
5. **Tapping it is tapping the threat** (AC 2 of the epic). A `pointerdown` inside a bubble's disc sends the same `PressThreat{threatId, t}` that a tap on the threat's icon would (same `t` from the event's `timeStamp`, same `press.pressed(pointerId, threatId)` so that the finger's `Release` follows wherever it lifts, same `onThreatPressed`, so the press highlight and `data-pp-press`); a held press on a Firmness bubble is judged at its release as on the icon; with Papa busy, it is sent and refused (`TapRefused`) exactly as on the icon. A bubble wins over whatever world point lies under it (D4). Nothing in the sim changes: the journal of a bubble tap is the journal of an icon tap.
6. **Keyboard and gamepad** (AC 2 of the epic, EXPERIENCE › Input Schemes). Auto-target is unchanged (off-screen threats are already candidates). When the targeted threat (or the pressed one) has a bubble, the bubble carries the target-highlight (a 4 px `{colors.alert}` outline outside its ink outline, as the marker's highlight), and the one glyph chip sits beside the bubble on the side toward the screen's centre, never in the bottom third and over no other bubble (D7). Space / A sends the `PressThreat` on it, as today.
7. **The silence stays without arrow or direction** (AC 3 of the epic, FR19 v2.3). In a `follow` level, while a silence runs (`world.silence.startedAt !== null`) its threat is `hidden` and only the `silence-bubble` « … » (and its ring) shows, with no arrow and no off-screen bubble; and the silence bubble no longer says where the zone is: it sits at the right edge just under the HUD row whatever the zone's side and height (D8). Once the baby is found (`SilenceEnded{found}`, the threat telegraphs at the zone), the threat gets an off-screen bubble if its icon is outside the frame, like any other. In a `fixed` level the silence bubble is exactly as before (side and height of the zone: `silenceBubble.test.ts` and `silence.spec.ts` unchanged and green).
8. **The HUD's node budget holds** (UX-DR26, EXPERIENCE › Performance Budget). With 3 bubbles up in `salon_kitchen` (and the level's whole HUD: gauges, timer, pause), `document.querySelectorAll('#pp-hud *').length <= HUD_MAX_DOM_NODES` (64: Geo raised it from 40 on 2026-11-04, the HUD having 36 nodes before any bubble). A bubble costs at most `OFFSCREEN_BUBBLE_MAX_NODES` (7) nodes with a circle icon (D5), and a hidden bubble costs none (mounted only while shown, like the silence ring). Pinned by a unit test (nodes per bubble, each shape) and by the e2e spec (the 64). If the measured peak does not fit, stop and tell Geo: never raise `HUD_MAX_DOM_NODES` on your own.
9. **Per frame, nothing allocates and the DOM is written on change only.** The layout runs on preallocated slots (3), the icons are built once per level (D5), the chevron's angle is quantised to whole degrees and written from a prebuilt string table, positions are whole px and written only when they change, the ring's per-mille only when it changes (as the marker's). No `getBoundingClientRect` per frame (the HUD row's bottom is measured on mount and on resize, as `silenceBubble` does).
10. **Debug mirror.** In mirrored builds only (`isMirrored`, `mirror()`), `data-pp-offscreen` lists the bubbles shown, sorted by threat id, `"<threatId>:<edge>:<xPx>:<yPx>:<angleDeg>"` joined by `;` (`edge` is `left | top | right`; `x`, `y` the bubble's centre in page px; empty string when none), written on change only. `ppOffscreen` joins `PROD_FORBIDDEN_MARKERS` in `tools/build/inspectDist.ts`, so `dist:check --flavor prod` proves it is not in the prod bundle and `--flavor playtest` that it is. `__PP__` gains no method (the « fourteen methods » pins stay).
11. **Nothing else moves.** No file under `src/sim`, `src/bots`, `content/` (except `strings.fr.json`), `content/_schemas/`, `tests/golden/` changes; `npm run golden:update` is not run. The fixed camera levels' e2e specs and the darwin screenshot references of `autoplay.spec.ts` pass unchanged. The marker's DOM is unchanged (the icon builder is extracted, not altered: `threatMarkers.test.ts` green as is).
12. **Geo can see it.** Screenshots in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/9-3-screenshots/`: (a) 1024 × 768, live feel, Papa at the kitchen end, the stairs threat off the right edge with its bubble; (b) 844 × 390 with a tighter frame (the e2e's `setFeel`), three bubbles, two of them on one edge; (c) 844 × 390, a silence in `salon_kitchen` (the neutral « … »); (d) the keyboard target on a bubble with its glyph chip.
