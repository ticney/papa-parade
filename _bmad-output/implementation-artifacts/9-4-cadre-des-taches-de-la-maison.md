# Story 9.4: Cadre des tâches de la maison

Status: review

<!-- Ultimate context engine analysis completed - comprehensive developer guide created. -->

## Story

As a player,
I want optional chores that play as parries,
so that I have something to do between two threats, at the risk of turning my back on the baby.

**Requirements covered:** FR76 (house chores: a station, beats judged like a parry with a chrono-ring and a 300 ms window, interruptible with progress kept, on hold during a threat's window, a finished chore fills the Maison row, a missed beat is a small gag), FR34 (the Maison row of Mama's note, 0 to 15, at last fed), FR73 (the `choreBeat` rise of the catastrophe gauge, +10 %), UX-DR27 (the chrono-ring on a beat), UX-DR28 (the activity bubble during a chore). **Depends on:** 3.12 (five-row note, `homePoints`, done), 3.16 (catastrophe gauge, `risePct.choreBeat`, done), 2.11 (chrono-ring helpers, done), 2.12 (activity bubble, done), 9.1 (the `stove` anchor in `salon_kitchen`, done), 9.2 (follow camera, done), 4.5 (the lullaby walk: the precedent of judged beats, done). 9.3 (off-screen bubble, done and merged: this branch starts after it).

**Scope:** the generic chore framework, end to end: a `content/chores/<id>.json` schema and its checks; a level declaring stations (`chores: [{ def, object }]` over an object of a new station kind); a sim system `src/sim/chores/` (start on arrival, beats announced and judged on the tap's timestamp, misses, the hold during a threat's window, leaving and resuming with progress kept, completion); Papa's new `chore` state and a glide to a station; the Maison row fed at level end; the catastrophe's `choreBeat` rise; the `perfect` and `human` bots doing chores, tuned in `bots.json`; the client (the station's marker with its chrono-ring, the beat tap, the activity bubble, the feedback cues, a debug mirror); **one real chore, `cooking`, on the `stove` of `salon_kitchen`**, so that it is playable and tested in the browser (D1).

**Not in this story:**
- What makes the cooking a cooking (9.5): the verbs of its four beats (remuer, écraser, remuer, couper le feu), the « purée qui attache » smoke gag, the « casserole sur le feu » disruption after 30 s abandoned. 9.4 ships the cooking's timing and points as data, and a generic miss cue; 9.5 dresses it. The `paused` state and its `enteredAt` (D4) are what 9.5's 30 s rule will read.
- The dishwasher (9.6): its station kind, its broken plate (Soupir, noise), the open door as a threat. 9.6 adds `dishwasher` to `CHORE_STATION_KINDS` and reads `paused` the same way.
- The `human` bot's off-screen visibility model and the 200-game balancing of `salon_kitchen` (9.8).
- An off-screen bubble for a station (9.3's deferred item, decided here: none, D13).
- Any change to the levels without stations (`salon_crawler`, `chambre_night`, the test levels): they play exactly as before, same journals, same events (AC 13).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **A chore is data** (AC 1 of the epic). `content/chores/<id>.json`, validated by a `ChoreDefSchema` (strict, `camelCase`, unit suffixes, no defaults): `id` (= file name), `station` (one of `CHORE_STATION_KINDS`: `['stove']` in this story), `windowMs` (`number | null`: `null` is `feel.timing.windowMs`, 300), `beats` (1 or more `{ telegraphDelayMs }`, whole ms, each greater than the base window), `homePoints` (≥ 0, halves allowed: 7.5), `feedback` (`telegraph`, `success`, `perfect`, `miss`, `completed`: cue ids of `feedback.json`). `npm run content:schemas` writes `content/_schemas/chore.schema.json`; `content:check` refuses an unknown cue, a beat delay not above the window, a station kind with no sim action, and a file whose `id` is not its name.
2. **A level declares its stations.** A level may have `chores: [{ def, object }]` (optional, **no default**: absent from every level that does not declare it, D2). Each `object` names an object of `level.objects` whose kind is the chore's `station`; an object is named by at most one chore; each object of a station kind is named by exactly one chore; the sum of the level's `homePoints` is at most `feel.mama.homeMaxPoints` (the level's `feelOverrides` applied). `content:check` and `simLevelOf` (`invariant`) both refuse a broken reference. `salon_kitchen` declares `{ "def": "cooking", "object": "stove" }` over a new object `{ "id": "stove", "kind": "stove", "anchor": "stove" }`.
3. **Tapping the station starts it** (AC 1 of the epic). A `TapObject` on a station whose chore is not done makes Papa **glide** to its anchor (`glideMaxMs`, 900 ms in `salon_kitchen`; D5) and, on arrival, enter the `chore` state: the chore goes `working` and its current beat is announced (`ChoreBeatTelegraphed`). While Papa carries the baby the tap is refused at once (`ChoreRefused`, Papa goes nowhere, a visible and audible cue: D10). A tap on a done station walks Papa there like any object and nothing starts.
4. **Each beat is judged like a parry** (AC 1 of the epic, D6). A beat announced at `T` opens its window at `T + telegraphDelayMs`; its window is `effectiveWindow(feel.timing, def.windowMs ?? timing.windowMs, { tier, fatigue, instinct })`, frozen at the announcement (the threats' rule). A `TapObject` on the station while Papa works there is the beat's press: `judgePress(t, windowOpenAt, window)` on the command's own `t` (never `world.time.sim`, ADR-003): `perfect` or `success` advances the chore to the next beat (`ChoreBeatJudged{quality}`); `late`, or `early` when `feel.input.earlyPress` is `fail`, is a miss (`ChoreBeatJudged{fail, reason}`); an `early` under `guard` is ignored. No tap by `windowOpenAt + windowMs + toleranceMs + input.lagMs` is a `late` miss. At most one beat is judged per step. A miss does not advance: the same beat is announced again at once.
5. **The beat has its chrono-ring** (AC 1 of the epic, UX-DR27). The station's marker in the world shows, while a beat is announced, the threats' chrono-ring (`chronoRingOf` from the beat's announcement to its window's close, the lit final arc, the perfect notch, sim time: frozen in pause and hitstop, slowed in slow-mo), built with the 2.11 helpers and `createChronoRingElement`; `ChronoArcLit` fires when its arc lights, as for a threat. No ring when no beat is announced (Papa away, the beat on hold, the chore done).
6. **Leaving keeps the progress** (AC 2 of the epic). While Papa works, any command that moves him (`PressThreat`, `TapFloor`, `TapBaby`, `TapObject` on another object, `Move`), a séquelle that occupies him, a sequence that starts, or taking the baby in his arms (a `TapBaby` within reach does not move him: `runChores` then sets him `idle`) takes him out of `chore`: the chore goes `paused` with its beats done kept, the announced beat is dropped without judgement. The next `TapObject` on that station glides him back and resumes at the first beat not done (`ChoreBeatTelegraphed` for it). A press on a threat during a chore is never refused (`TapRefused` never fires for it: a chore is not a busy occupation).
7. **A threat's window holds the beat** (AC 3 of the epic, D7). While any threat is `windowOpen`, the working chore's beat is `held`: an announced beat is withdrawn without judgement and its ring hides. The step no threat is `windowOpen` any more, the beat is announced again from scratch (`ChoreBeatTelegraphed`, a full `telegraphDelayMs`). Arriving at the station while a threat's window is open starts the chore held.
8. **A finished chore pays at the end** (AC 4 of the epic). When the last beat is judged `perfect` or `success`, the chore goes `done` (`ChoreCompleted{homePoints}`), Papa goes `idle` at the station, and the station is spent for the level. At level end, `scoreLevel` passes the sum of the done chores' `homePoints` to `computeMamaScore` (which holds it to `homeMaxPoints`): the Maison row of the Result screen shows it (`7,5 / 15` with `cooking` done) and `LevelScored.homePoints` carries it. An unfinished chore is worth 0. A level without stations still shows `0 / 15`.
9. **A missed beat is a gag that worries Mama** (AC 4 of the epic). A missed beat plays its chore's `miss` cue (sound, visual, haptic) and raises the catastrophe by `feel.catastrophe.risePct.choreBeat` (10) × the tier's `catastropheMultiplier`, source `choreBeat` (`CatastropheChanged`), read by `runCatastrophe` from this step's `ChoreBeatJudged{fail}` (D9). A `perfect` beat lowers it by `perfectDropPct`, source `perfect`. In the final rush the gauge stays frozen, as for every source. At 100 % Mama comes home as for any other rise.
10. **Beats count as parries for Rire** (D8). A `perfect` beat adds to `world.score.perfects`, a `success` to `world.score.successes` (Rire: 1 and 0.5 points). Beats touch neither the combo nor the instinct, and cause no hitstop and no slow-mo.
11. **The activity bubble shows the chore** (UX-DR28, D11). While Papa is in `chore`, the activity bubble above him shows the station's pictogram and a ring that **fills** with the chore's progress (beats done / beats), not a drain to `busyUntil`; it pops in and out as for any occupation; no tap is refused, so it never shakes. `isPapaBusy('chore')` is `false`.
12. **The bots do chores, tuned in `bots.json`** (AC 5 of the epic, D12). `bots.json` gains `chores: { isEnabled, quietMinMs }` (both bots) and `human.backTurnedReactionMs`. With `isEnabled`, `perfect` starts or resumes a chore when Papa is free and no threat has been active for `quietMinMs`, taps each beat at its window's centre, and leaves for any threat as today. On `salon_kitchen`, over 30 seeds, `perfect` finishes `cooking` in every run with **0 threat failed** (today's report: 0 gags on 30 runs) and a mean score above today's 79.7. `human` taps beats with its `withChronoRing` read error and reacts `backTurnedReactionMs` later to a telegraph while it works at a station. `npm run sim`'s report gains a `chores` section (runs completed, beats by quality, mean Maison points, mean beats per run).
13. **Nothing else moves.** On every level without stations: same events, same journals, same `feelHash` and `levelHash` (no new feel key, no defaulted level key, no new required key in `LevelContent`), no new draw of `world.rng` or of a bot's RNG; `tests/bots/humanBotRegression.test.ts` passes **unchanged**. The goldens are regenerated **deliberately** (`npm run golden:update`) because `World` gains `chores` and the snapshot goes to version 32: their event streams must be byte-identical before and after (only world hashes and the snapshot version may move); say so in the Completion Notes. `#pp-hud`'s node count does not grow.
14. **Debug mirror and test hooks.** In mirrored builds only, `data-pp-chore` lists the level's chores, by index, `"<index>:<state>:<beatsDone>/<beatCount>:<beatState>"` joined by `;` (empty when the level has none), written on change. `ppChore` joins `PROD_FORBIDDEN_MARKERS` in `tools/build/inspectDist.ts`. `__PP__` gains no method (the « fourteen methods » pins stay).
15. **Geo can see it.** Screenshots in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/9-4-screenshots/`: (a) 844 × 390, Papa at the stove, a beat announced with its ring and the activity bubble; (b) the ring's lit arc; (c) the Result screen with `Maison 7,5 / 15`. Say in the notes what was checked by eye and what only Geo can judge (whether the beat reads at the station, the station's icon, the bubble's filling ring, the timing of the four beats). The merge waits for Geo's OK on the feel.
