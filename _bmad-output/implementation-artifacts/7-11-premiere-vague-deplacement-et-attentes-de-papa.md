## Story

As a player,
I want Papa to set off, brake, walk on tiptoe and keep himself busy while he waits,
so that he looks like a real dad and not a game piece.

**Requirements covered:** FR79 (the first wave of Papa's panel: the start, the brake, the tiptoe with puffed cheeks, three idles), NFR2 (a tap's visual feedback stays within 50 ms). **Depends on:** the v2 skeleton and the headless Blender pipeline of 7.9 (`papa.blend`, `export_character.py`, the floor and hinge guards), the life layer of 7.8 (`feel.json › characters`, `LifeInputs`, the passage fades, the lean), the guard of the signals of 7.10 (`isSignalOnly`, `faceInputs.ts`), the stealth exit of 4.9 (`world.sequence.stealth`), the event → one-shot path of 7.2 (`playCharacterEvent`).

**Scope:** `papa.blend` gains six clips (`walk_start`, `walk_stop`, `tiptoe`, `idle_scratch`, `idle_neck`, `idle_stretch`) and one hidden node (`papa_cheeks`), within today's triangle and draw-call ceilings; a pure module schedules Papa's idles from the level's seed and the sim clock; `papaView` plays a start, a brake or an idle as a cuttable one-shot and swaps `walk` for `tiptoe` in a stealth step; the rig gains a way to cut a one-shot in the same frame; the cadence lives in `feel.json › characters`. No sim change.

**Not in this story:**
- Any change to `src/sim`, `src/bots`, `content/threats`, `content/sequences`, `content/levels`, the room `.blend` files and `public/assets/levels/`, `public/_headers`, `tools/blender/export_level.py`, `baby.blend` / `baby.glb`.
- The rest of the first wave: stirring the pot, picking up a debris, the second Parry, the sigh of relief, the baby sitting, clapping and his second laugh (all 7.12); the panicked run and the shuffling walk (7.14); idles at a chore station (D9).
- Carried variants of the start, the brake or the idles (the deferred « only `idle` and `walk` have carried variants » stays open).
- Moving the life layer to `pelvis` / `neck` (7.9's D2 stays Geo's option).

## Acceptance Criteria

The epics' criteria (`epics.md › Story 7.11`), made testable. The decisions D1-D15 below are part of them.

1. **Six clips and a node in the file (D1, D2).** `papa.glb` holds `walk_start`, `walk_stop`, `tiptoe`, `idle_scratch`, `idle_neck`, `idle_stretch` at the lengths of the clip sheet (30 fps), and a hidden node `papa_cheeks`. `PAPA_CONTRACT.clips` gains the six, `PAPA_CONTRACT.hiddenNodes` gains `papa_cheeks`, and every guard of `characterAssets.test.ts` holds on the new file: the D1 skeleton (22 joints, unchanged), the ceilings `PAPA_VISIBLE_TRIANGLES_7_8` (2,600), `PAPA_ALL_TRIANGLES_7_8` (4,416 with every hidden node, so the cheeks cost at most 50 triangles or are paid for elsewhere), 4 draw calls, 6 materials; feet on the floor (±0.03 m at 9 samples) in the six clips; one-way elbows and knees; no track on a code-driven bone or node (`eyelids`, `brow_*`, `forelock`, `papa_mug_steam`, `papa_blabla`, and no key on `eyes`, which stays `coffee_kick`'s); every one-shot at least `2 × fades.returnMs`; `papa.glb` under `CHARACTER_GLB_MAX_BYTES`; `npm run dist:check -- --flavor playtest` passes (NFR15).
2. **A start and a brake (D3, D4).** When Papa's loop goes from `idle` to `walk` after he stood still at least `startStop.startMinStillMs`, `walk_start` plays over the walk loop, fading in over `fades.startMs`; when it goes from `walk` to `idle` after he walked at least `startStop.brakeMinTravelMs`, `walk_stop` plays over the idle loop, fading in over `fades.otherMs`. Each hands back to its loop as any one-shot does (`returnMs`). His position is the sim's at every frame (the view moves no position); the life layer's lean still lies over both (7.8). No start or brake while the baby is carried, into or out of a `glide`, during a stealth or duel step (the guard, D7), or under reduced motion.
3. **Tiptoe and puffed cheeks in the stealth exit (D5, D6).** While `isStealthStepRunning(world)`, the loop that would be `walk` is `tiptoe`, played at its pace from `feedback.json › characters.clipSpeeds` (a new row, `tiptoe` at the stealth speed); outside a stealth step nothing plays `tiptoe`. While `world.sequence.stealth.breathHeldAt !== null`, `papa_cheeks` is shown, and only then; it stays shown under reduced motion (it carries a state). `fx()` gains `papaCheeksShown`.
4. **Three idles on a cadence (D8, D9, D10).** While Papa is `idle`, not carrying, outside the guard, not under reduced motion, his idles come at the slots of D8: the first `idles.firstAfterMs` (4000) after `papa.enteredAt`, then one every `idles.gapMinMs` to `idles.gapMaxMs` (5000 to 8000) start to start, each one of `idle_scratch`, `idle_neck`, `idle_stretch` and never the same as the slot before it. A slot plays its idle in the first frame whose clock reaches it, if Papa is eligible then and no other one-shot is on; otherwise that slot shows nothing and the next comes on time. The cadence is `feel.json › characters.idles`, read every frame.
5. **A tap or a state change cuts it, in that frame (D11, D12).** A running idle, start or brake is cut in the frame that sees any of: a change of Papa's loop, a `PapaStateChanged`, or an event of `PAPA_ACTING_CUT_EVENTS` (the events an accepted command produces, D12). In that frame `fx().papaClip` is no longer the cut clip and the loop the state says starts fading in over `fades.startMs` (into a travelling loop) or `fades.otherMs` (otherwise), never `stopMs` or `returnMs`. A response, a gag, a coffee kick or any other event one-shot replaces it at once (as today). A headless test proves that every command a player can send to an idle Papa (`TapFloor`, `TapBaby`, `TapObject`, `Move`, `PressThreat`) yields, in the step that applies it, a `PapaStateChanged` or an event of the list. The tap's visual feedback is untouched: `tests/e2e/tapLatency.spec.ts` passes as it is.
6. **No idle where only the signals count, or with the baby in his arms (D7, D9).** During a duel or stealth step (`isSignalOnly(world)`: the bedtime duel, the bomb drop, the stealth exit, so every feint), with the baby `carried`, in any state other than `idle` (`chore` included, D9), or under reduced motion, no idle plays and Papa keeps his loop; a running idle is cut when one of these begins.
7. **Reproducible (D8, D13).** The slots and the choice are a pure function of the level's seed (`SceneBodies.levelSeed`), `papa.enteredAt` and the sim clock: no `Math.random`, real time, `Date`, `performance` or `world.rng` (the scans of `characterAssets.test.ts` cover the new module). The view keeps no memory of the last idle: it recomputes it from the same inputs, so a highlight replay from a snapshot (story 3.9) and the end photo show the same idles at the same slots. The same game played twice gives the same idles at the same slots; 1, 3 or 7 frames over the same span play the same idles at the same slots; a `dtMs` of 0 starts nothing; `resetFx()` cuts whatever runs.
8. **Data, hot (D10).** `characters.idles { firstAfterMs, gapMinMs, gapMaxMs }` and `characters.startStop { startMinStillMs, brakeMinTravelMs }` live in `feel.json › characters` (client only), with Zod bounds, a `refine` that `gapMinMs <= gapMaxMs`, a `describe` on every key and group, the regenerated `content/_schemas/feel.schema.json`, every key passing the unit-suffix rule of `tests/content/feelSchema.test.ts`, and are read from `world.feel.characters` every frame (a change in the feel panel shows on the next frame).

10. **Nothing looks like a telegraph (D2).** No new clip keys `eyelids` or `eyes` (so his lids stay open outside the micro-sleep); no idle yawns (no hand to the mouth, the head not thrown back) or sags his head like the `asleep` loop: in every idle at 9 samples, the head's up axis leans at most `IDLE_HEAD_LEAN_MAX_DEG` (25°) from vertical and less than the `asleep` loop's own maximum on the committed file (both numbers in the notes).

## Dev Notes

### Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Six clips under these names** (the clip sheet below): `walk_start` and `walk_stop` (one-shots), `tiptoe` (a loop), `idle_scratch`, `idle_neck`, `idle_stretch` (one-shots). The idles and the start / brake are « acting » one-shots (`PAPA_ACTING_CLIPS`): they decorate a state and are cut by anything (D11); event one-shots (gags, `stumble`, `coffee_kick`, responses, the finale) are not acting and keep their rules. | The GDD's panel (« Déplacement », « Attente », ⭐). One-shots over the loop reuse the rig's fades and hand-back; a separate « acting » list lets the view cut only what never carries a state (principle 3: « un tap coupe sur-le-champ toute attente, toute réaction et tout démarrage »). |
| D2 | **The cheeks are a hidden node, `papa_cheeks`**, as `papa_look_mash`: a separate mesh in the skin colour (no new material: Papa is at 6 = `CHARACTER_MATERIALS_MAX`), weighted 100 % to `head`, hidden by default, shown by the view. Triangles: `PAPA_ALL_TRIANGLES_7_8` (4,416) does not move; 4,366 are used, so two puffs fit in 50 triangles (e.g. two 6 × 3 half-spheres), or pay from a hidden prop. **Nothing like a telegraph:** no new clip keys `eyelids` or `eyes`, no idle brings a hand to the mouth or throws the head back (no yawn), none sags the head like `asleep` (AC 10's measure). | Epics: « un nœud caché, montré par la vue comme les parties de la dégaine ». GDD principle 4: « hors micro-sieste, les paupières de papa ne tombent jamais »; the micro-sleep's telegraph is his lids, his sleep the `asleep` slump. |
| D3 | **The start.** In `animate`, when the loop changes from `idle` to `walk` (not `carry_*`, not `tiptoe`, not `glide`), Papa stood still at least `startStop.startMinStillMs` before it (the sim time between his last travelling state's end and `papa.enteredAt` of the walk, read from the world), the guard is off and motion is not reduced: `rig.play('walk', …)` as today, then `playOnce('walk_start', fades.startMs)`. The clip plays at time scale 1, the walk loop runs under it at its pace, and it hands back over `returnMs`. Its anticipation fits in its first 2-3 frames: the sim moves him from the first step and « rien ne retarde une réponse ». | Epics: « un démarrage avec élan … qui s'ajoute à l'élan de la couche de vie ; sa position reste celle de la sim ». A glide is a response: nothing may dress it up. A start after a 100 ms stop would stutter, hence the floor. |
| D4 | **The brake.** When the loop changes from `walk` to `idle` after a walk of at least `startStop.brakeMinTravelMs` (from the first travelling state's `enteredAt` to the stop), outside the guard and reduced motion, with no carried baby: `rig.play('idle', …)`, then `playOnce('walk_stop', fades.otherMs)`. Only into `idle`: an arrival into `drink`, `onPhone`, `chore`, `pickingUp`, a pick-up (`carry_idle`) or a response pose plays that state's clip as today.
