## Story

As a player,
I want Papa to stir the pot, pick up the debris, parry in more than one way and sigh when he scrapes through, and the baby to sit down, clap his hands and laugh in more than one way,
so that the game stops repeating the same gestures.

**Requirements covered:** FR79 (the second half of the first wave: four clips for Papa, four for the baby), FR68 (the playtest indicator « Papa se lit sans sa bulle »). **Depends on:** the acting of 7.11 (`papaActing.ts`: `actingMix32`, `idleSlotAt`, the salts; `PAPA_ACTING_CLIPS`, `PAPA_ACTING_CUT_EVENTS`, `characterRig.cutOnce`, the clip sheet and headless Blender pipeline), the guard of 7.10 (`isSignalOnly`, `faceInputs.ts`), the life layer of 7.8 (`LifeInputs`, the passage fades, `feel.json › characters`), the v2 skeletons of 7.9, the chores of 9.4 / 9.5 (`chore` state, `world.chores[i].state === 'working'`), the debris of 3.15 (`pickingUp`, `busyUntil`), the response choreography (`responseChoreography.ts`, P3), the activity bubble of 2.12 and the playtest tool of 3.4.

**Scope:** `papa.blend` gains `stir_pot`, `pick_up`, `respond_parry_sweep`, `sigh_relief`; `baby.blend` gains `crawl_pause`, `sit_down`, `clap`, `laugh_belly`, all within today's triangle, draw-call and byte ceilings. The views play them from the world and the events: the stir at the cooking station (a new `feedback.json` table), the pick-up fitted to the sim's busy span, the two Parries alternating on `world.score.successes`, the sigh after a success in the last quarter of its window, the baby's pause, sit and clap on the 7.11 schedule, the two laughs alternating. Three small feel blocks. A dev / playtest `?bubble=off` switch, and a sixth indicator in `npm run playtest:metrics`. No sim change.

**Not in this story:**
- Any change to `src/sim`, `src/bots`, `content/threats`, `content/sequences`, `content/levels`, `content/chores`, the room `.blend` files and `public/assets/levels/`, `public/_headers`, `tools/blender/export_level.py`.
- The dishwasher's gesture and tasting the purée (7.14: the dishwasher station keeps `idle`); the variants of Firmness and of the perfect, the threats' own gestures and the combo pose (7.13); the facepalm and the look to camera after a gag (7.14); the baby's toes, thumb, third laugh, pout, applause, yawn and pointing (7.15).
- A spoon or any new node (D2), a « get up » clip for the baby (D7), a laugh counter in the sim (D10).
- `npm run ci` and the playtest link: the animation playtest comes after this story, on Geo's call (`sprint-change-proposal-2026-11-08.md`).

## Acceptance Criteria

The epics' criteria (`epics.md › Story 7.12`), made testable. The decisions D1-D17 below are part of them.

1. **Eight clips in the files (D1, D11).** `papa.glb` holds `stir_pot`, `pick_up`, `respond_parry_sweep`, `sigh_relief`, and `baby.glb` holds `crawl_pause`, `sit_down`, `clap`, `laugh_belly`, at the lengths of the clip sheet (30 fps). The contracts gain them; `PAPA_PARRY_CLIPS`, `BABY_IDLE_CLIPS`, `BABY_ACTING_CLIPS`, `BABY_LAUGH_CLIPS` are exported and `PAPA_ACTING_CLIPS` gains `sigh_relief`. Every guard of `characterAssets.test.ts` holds on both new files: the skeletons (22 and 19 joints, unchanged), the triangle ceilings (`PAPA_VISIBLE_TRIANGLES_7_8`, `PAPA_ALL_TRIANGLES_7_8`, `BABY_TRIANGLES_7_8`: no node is added), the draw calls and materials, feet on the floor (±0.03 m at 9 samples) in every new loop and acting one-shot, one-way hinges, no track on a code-driven bone or node, every one-shot at least `2 × fades.returnMs`, each `.glb` under `CHARACTER_GLB_MAX_BYTES`, and `npm run dist:check -- --flavor playtest` passes (NFR15).
2. **He stirs the pot (D2).** While Papa is in `chore` at a station whose chore def has a row in `feedback.json › characters.choreClips` (`cooking` → `stir_pot`), his loop is that clip instead of `idle`; at `unload_dishwasher` (no row) it stays `idle`. Content checks: every row names an existing chore def and a clip of `PAPA_CONTRACT`, one row per chore.
3. **He picks the debris up at the sim's pace (D3).** While Papa is `pickingUp`, his loop is `pick_up`, at a time scale that makes one cycle last `busyUntil − enteredAt` (clamped to `[PICK_UP_TIME_SCALE_MIN, PICK_UP_TIME_SCALE_MAX]`); the sim's duration (`pickUpMs`) does not change.
4. **Two Parries, never the same twice in a row (D4).** A judged Parry success plays `PAPA_PARRY_CLIPS[(successes + seedBit) mod 2]`, where `successes` is `world.score.successes` in the step of its `ResponseResolved` and `seedBit` is drawn once from the level's seed by `actingMix32`: two Parry successes with no other success between them always differ. Both keep the Parry silhouette (a hand forward at the peak, AC 13). In a duel or stealth step, `respond_parry` alone. No `Math.random`, real time or `world.rng`.
5. **A sigh when he scrapes through (D5, D6).** A `ResponseResolved` of quality `success` whose `offsetMs` lies in the last `relief.lastSharePct` % of the window *with its hidden tolerance* (`(offsetMs + half) / (2 × half) ≥ 1 − lastSharePct / 100`, `half = responseWindow.windowMs / 2 + responseWindow.toleranceMs`) arms a sigh when its response pose plays; never for a perfect, a fail, a `null` offset, an early-side success, or in a duel or stealth step. The sigh (`sigh_relief`) plays once his response pose has handed back, Papa `idle` with no other one-shot; it is an acting one-shot (a tap or a state change cuts it in that frame, D11 of 7.11). It is dropped, never played late, when his state changes after it was armed, when any event of `PAPA_ACTING_CUT_EVENTS` or a `GagTriggered` comes later, when the baby is carried, when the guard begins, when the level's flow is no longer live, when a deferred finale one-shot is pending, and by `resetFx()`. The share is `feel.json › characters.relief.lastSharePct`, read when the response is judged.
6. **The baby pauses, then sits (D7).** While the baby is in `babble`, not crying, outside the guard and with motion not reduced, his loop is `crawl_pause` until `babySit.afterMs` after `baby.enteredAt`, then `babble` (sitting, today's clip), the passage playing `sit_down` over it. The moment the sim sets him off (any other state), his acting one-shot is cut in that frame and the new loop fades in over `fades.startMs` into a travelling loop (`otherMs` otherwise): his drawn position is the sim's at every frame, and on every step where he is drawn paused or seated he did not crawl (AC 12: his sim position did not change, bar the sim's instant snap onto an open cell, D7). In a duel or stealth step, while crying, in `downstairs`, or under reduced motion, his loop is today's (`babble` from the first frame), with no `crawl_pause`, no `sit_down`, no clap.
7. **He claps, on a cadence (D8).** While seated (`babble` after `babySit.afterMs`, the sit-down over), not crying, with no one-shot, outside the guard and with motion not reduced, the baby claps at the slots of `idleSlotAt` with `BABY_IDLE_SALT`, anchored on `baby.enteredAt`, the cadence `feel.json › characters.babyIdles`: the first `firstAfterMs` after `enteredAt`, then every `gapMinMs` to `gapMaxMs`, start to start. A slot he is not free for shows nothing and the next comes on time. 1, 3 or 7 frames over the same span clap at the same slots; a `dtMs` of 0 starts nothing; `resetFx()` cuts whatever runs.
8. **Two laughs, never the same twice in a row (D10).** Each time `babyOneShotOf` gives `laugh` (a pick-up, a gag whose `babyClip` is `laugh`), the baby plays one of `BABY_LAUGH_CLIPS`, never the one he played last; the first of a level (or after `resetFx()`) is drawn from the level's seed. In a duel or stealth step, `laugh` alone and the alternation does not move. A game played twice from its start gives the same laughs in the same order.
9. **Data, hot (D12).** `characters.babySit { afterMs }`, `characters.babyIdles { firstAfterMs, gapMinMs, gapMaxMs }` and `characters.relief { lastSharePct }` live in `feel.json › characters` (client only), strict, bounded on both sides, a `refine` `gapMinMs <= gapMaxMs` on `babyIdles`, a `describe` on every key and group, the unit-suffix rule of `tests/content/feelSchema.test.ts`, `content/_schemas/feel.schema.json` regenerated, read from `world.feel.characters` every frame (the relief share at the judgement). `feedback.json › characters.choreClips` is a strict table checked by its schema (one row per chore).
10. **`?bubble=off` (D13).** In a dev or playtest build, `?bubble=off` keeps Papa's activity bubble undrawn for the whole session (the shake on a refused tap included), and nothing else changes: the clips, the cues, the combo badge (centred, as with no bubble), the off-screen and silence bubbles, the journal. Any other value is ignored with one `warn`. A production build ignores it (the `isMirrored` gate of `?threats=off`). A browser test (extended tier) proves it.
11. **The sixth indicator (D14).** `npm run playtest:metrics` computes « Papa se lit sans sa bulle »: per activity of `content/playtest.json › bodyRead.activities` (`chore`, `coffee`, `phone`, `stealth`, `fatigue`, `carrying`), the share of the testers with a note who named it, OK at `namedMinPct` (80) for every activity with data; and the testers who spontaneously found a pose repetitive, OK at `repetitivePoseMaxTesters` (0) or fewer. Geo's notes take three optional columns (`activityId`, `activityNamed`, `repetitivePoseCited`): a CSV without them still loads, indicator 6 is then `n/a` with one warning. The report (Markdown and JSON, version 3) shows both rows, a per-activity table and each tester's notes; `observations.example.csv` and `observation-sheet.md` say how to fill them.
12. **Reproducible, and the game does not move (D15).** The pause, the sit, the claps, the Parries, the pick-up span and the sighs are functions of the world, the events, the level's seed and the sim clock (the laughs: of the events in order, D10), never of `Math.random`, real time, `Date`, `performance` or `world.rng` (the scans of `characterAssets.test.ts` cover the new modules). No file changes under « Not in this story ». `npm run golden:update` is run once, deliberately: only the `feelHash` and `worldHash` lines of `tests/golden/journals/` move, with D15's proof. `npm run sim` with the four bots on the three levels, seed 1, gives the same reports as `main` apart from the hashes. A headless live test proves AC 4, 6, 7 on the live levels.
13. **Nothing looks like a telegraph or gives an answer away (D11).** No new clip keys a lid, Papa's `eyes` or the baby's `head`; `sigh_relief` and `stir_pot` keep his head within `IDLE_HEAD_LEAN_MAX_DEG` (25°) of vertical and below `asleep`'s lean; both Parries reach forward at their peak at least `PARRY_REACH_MIN_SHARE` (80 %) of `respond_parry`'s reach; the baby's head never rises above his own `babble` maximum + 0.03 m in `crawl_pause`, `sit_down`, `clap` (above `laugh`'s + 0.03 m in `laugh_belly`) (he never pulls himself up); `crawl_pause` moves his `neck` through at least 8° over its loop (a still head reads as a stare); no new baby clip moves his `root` more than 0.05 m sideways (he never darts). No file under `views/` reads `windowOpenAt`, `feintAt`, `realWakePct` or `toleranceMs`; the one new reader of `toleranceMs`, `src/client/feedback/reliefSigh.ts`, reads it only from a `ResponseResolved`'s `responseWindow` and takes no threat.
14. **Geo can see them and edit them (D17).** Captures in `7-12-screenshots/`: in game at 844 × 390 (DPR 2), Papa stirring, mid pick-up, both Parries at their peaks, the sigh; the baby paused on all fours, mid sit-down, clapping, both laughs; pose sheets of the eight clips (Workbench, five frames each). `notes.txt` lists each image, its clip and sim time, what you saw, and every image not opened.
