## Story

As a player,
I want to empty the dishwasher at the right rhythm,
so that I earn Maison points without breaking a plate.

**Requirements covered:** FR76 (the second house chore: « vider le lave-vaisselle », four beats « rattraper l'assiette qui glisse du panier », spaced 1.5 to 3 s; a missed beat is a small gag, « assiette cassée (Soupir et bruit) »; « un lave-vaisselle laissé ouvert devient une cible du bébé »), FR78 (the open dishwasher, from the GDD's candidate list: « Lave-vaisselle ouvert | Cuisine | Parer : refermer la porte | Bébé assis dans le panier, couverts au sol | +20 % »), FR34 (the Maison row reaches 15 with both chores, 7.5 each; the Soupir row counts the broken plates at « 4 par objet cassé »), FR73 (the catastrophe: +10 % per missed beat, +20 % for the open-door gag). **Depends on:** 9.4 (house chores: done, merged at `03e7630`), **9.5 (cooking verbs, `verbKey` per beat, the `gagNoisePct` / `NoiseSource 'gag'` precedent, the « own threat holds the chore » rule, the mess marked at the threat's point: must be merged into `main` before this story is developed, see T0)**, 9.3 (off-screen bubble), 9.2 (follow camera), 3.16 (catastrophe), 3.15 (séquelles, `mess`), 3.12 (five-row note), 3.13 (fairness), 3.1 (noise gauge).

**Scope:** the dishwasher of `salon_kitchen`, end to end.
1. **A second station kind, `dishwasher`**, and its chore `unload_dishwasher`: four beats « RATTRAPE ! » (catch the plate sliding out of the rack), 7.5 Maison points.
2. **A missed beat breaks a plate**: one broken object for Mama's Soupir (−4), noise (+10 %), the catastrophe's `choreBeat` rise (+10 %, unchanged), a crash cue, and shards left on the floor in front of the dishwasher (on the photo too).
3. **The open dishwasher is a target of the baby**: while the chore is started and Papa has left it, its door is open, and the director may draw the wave threat `dishwasher_open` (« REFERME ! », a parry). Caught, Papa closes the door: the baby cannot use it again until Papa reopens it. Missed: « bébé assis dans le panier, couverts au sol » (a mischief for the Soupir, the mess left on the floor, +20 %).
4. **Two stations make the 9.4 deferred items real**: two chores never `working` at once; the bots walk the list of chores; the activity bubble's pictogram follows the station (a plate at the dishwasher).

**Not in this story:**
- The other new mischiefs of the big room (tablecloth, low cabinet, bin, plant, Papa's mug): 9.7. The open dishwasher is 9.6's because the epic gives it to this story (AC 2 of 9.6) and it depends on the chore; 9.7 must not plan it again.
- Moving the `dishwasher_door` node (opening, closing), Papa's catching pose, the baby sitting in the rack: E7's art (`deferred-work.md`, 9.1: « The E9 moving pieces are static nodes »). The door's state is a sim fact the client may read later.
- The `human` bot's off-screen visibility model and the 200-game balance of `salon_kitchen` (9.8).
- Any change to the levels without a dishwasher (`salon_crawler`, `chambre_night`, the test levels): same events, same journals, same hashes (AC 13).
