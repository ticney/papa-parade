# Story 9.8: Niveau grand salon jouable de bout en bout

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As Geo,
I want the big room as a complete level, tested by the bots,
so that I can hand it to the playtest of the third prototype.

**Requirements covered:** FR31 (« les menaces s'empilent par vagues. Vague 1 : une à la fois. Vague 2 : deux simultanées. Vague 3 : trois simultanées et une perturbation », windows spaced by the fairness rule), FR66 (« le jeu peut être lancé et joué de bout en bout sans intervention humaine »), FR77 (« un grand salon avec cuisine ouverte … **remplace le salon** », the follow camera, the off-screen bubble). GDD indicator « La défaite est possible sans être un mur » (gdd.md:629): « En parties simulées au palier Normal : un joueur simulé humain … perd le niveau dans 10 à 30 % des parties ; un joueur simulé parfait ne le perd jamais ; un joueur simulé inactif le perd avant la fin du chrono. **Le rythme des menaces se règle pour tenir ces cibles** ». Architecture « Automatisation » (game-architecture.md:285): « **Modèle de visibilité (E9) :** avec la caméra qui suit, il ne réagit à une menace hors cadre qu'après l'apparition de sa bulle ; le cadre est recalculé depuis la position de papa et `feel.camera`, sans `three` » and ADR-011 (:316, « vérifié par le bot `perfect` sur 200 graines : 0 raté »). **Depends on:** 9.1–9.7, all merged (`main` at `533975d`, « Story 9.7 : merge de story/9-7-nouvelles-betises-du-grand-salon »). **This story completes Epic 9**: the merge needs a green `npm run ci` (CLAUDE.md), which is also the epic's « `npm run ci` est vert avant le lien de playtest ».

**Scope:** `salon_kitchen` becomes the game's first level, end to end.
1. **The first level.** `DEFAULT_LEVEL_ID` becomes `salon_kitchen`: the start screen, a production build, `npm run sim` without `--level` and a restart all play the big room. `salon_crawler` stays a level (`?level=salon_crawler`) and stays the e2e suite's test room, explicitly.
2. **The `human` bot's visibility model.** In a `follow` level, the bot rebuilds the follow frame from Papa's position and `feel.camera` (the pure maths of `src/shared/followCamera.ts` and `followVisibility.ts`, written for this story by 9.2 and 9.3) and reacts to a threat only from the moment it is in the frame or has an off-screen bubble (at most 3, the most urgent). A `fixed` level plays exactly as before.
3. **The 200-game balance.** The waves of `salon_kitchen.json` (and only the big room's own data) are retuned so that, over 200 seeds at the Normal tier, `human` loses 10–30 % of its games (`mamaHome`), `perfect` fails nothing, never loses and finishes both chores every time, and `idle` loses before the timer. A new `npm run sim:balance` proves it from targets kept in `content/playtest.json`, and `npm run ci` runs it.
4. **The room is frozen.** From this story on, `salon_kitchen.geometry.json` follows the 7.1 rule of the salon and the bedroom (an art pass never changes it).

**Not in this story:**
- The deployment of the playtest link (`wrangler`, Geo's first login), Geo's device pass (`docs/playtest/device-measurements.md`) and the playtest itself: after the merge, with Geo (CLAUDE.md › Playtest deployment). This story leaves `npm run ci` green and the playtest checklist ready (AC 13).
- Age selection, the Fridge, unlocking, the implicit tutorial's other rules: 6.9 / 3.11. There is no level list or age in the code: « first level of the baby age » is `DEFAULT_LEVEL_ID`.
- A bedtime `cycle` in `salon_kitchen` (its sequences stay debug starts, as in the salon: the cycle is `chambre_night`'s). The 9.4 deferred item « the bedtime cycle gathers only an `idle` Papa » therefore stays latent.
- The follow's aim at the judged threat during a slow-mo, in the bot's frame (D4), the perfect's zoom, lean and turn (9.3 D2, already left out of the client's own test).
- Moving an anchor of the big room, renaming the `papa_mug` level node, any art: question 4.
- Any change to `salon_crawler.json`, `chambre_night.json`, `content/feel.json`, the threat files the salon shares (`outlet`, `stairs`, `coffee_table_object`, `silence_mischief`, `papa_microsleep`, `arms_cry`, the sequences' threats) or the existing `human` knobs of `bots.json` (AC 12).
