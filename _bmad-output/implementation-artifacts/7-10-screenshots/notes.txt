Story 7.10 — the faces, captures (D13)

How: a throwaway Playwright file (deleted), Chromium, 844 × 390 CSS px, deviceScaleFactor 2, the game's
DPR cap 2. `?threats=off` for the expression sets, `__PP__.freeze`, `__PP__.setExpressions` (the overlay),
`advance` by 5 steps until fx()'s two brow tilts stop changing, then the screenshot. Each `<name>.png` is
the full frame; each `<name>_crop4x.png` is cut from that same PNG (no re-render), 50 × 50 CSS px around
Papa's brows (left panel) and the baby's (right panel), ×4 nearest neighbour, magenta between them. The
drawn pixel ratio (canvas.width / clientWidth) is 2 for every image but one (said below).

T0 measure (before any code, `physicalPxPerM` from the live camera along screen right at the brows' height):
84 physical px per metre on both levels at DPR 2. Papa's bar is 7 cm × 3 cm → 5.9 × 2.5 px; the baby's
6.7 cm × 2.5 cm → 5.6 × 2.1 px. Both under the ~6 px of D10, so `browScalePct` starts at 120 for both
(Papa 7.0 px, the baby 6.7 px long). Not retuned after the captures: the look is Geo's call.

Setups:
- `<level>_papa_<expression>_after`: Papa idle, walked toward the camera so that he faces it, the baby
  sitting (a long babble, `baby.babbleMinMs/MaxMs` 20 s through `setFeel`) behind him past
  `gaze.behindDeg`, so his head looks ahead. The baby is `neutral` in these.
- `<level>_baby_<expression>_after`: the baby carried (`TapBaby`), Papa walked toward the camera, so the baby
  faces it; Papa's face is hidden behind the baby here. Papa is `neutral` in these.
- `<level>_neutral_before`: untouched code (before T1), Papa beside the baby, both `idle`/`crawl`.

Natural moments (no overlay, threats on, `fx()` names the face): the baby right after `BabyPickedUp`
(delighted, his brows still blending: 5.6° of 6°), and Papa right after the first `ThreatTelegraphed`
(surprised, 9.7° of 10°; the baby `focused` on that threat). The surprise was drawn at a pixel ratio of 1.5:
the game stepped its cap down after two slow 2 s windows of the headless GPU (`adaptivePixelRatio.ts`).
In both, the face is turned away from the camera at that instant (Papa's head toward the threat, the carried
baby along Papa's heading, away from us): they show the moment's timing, not the pose. The pose sets above
show the faces.

What was seen, on the ×4 crops: Papa facing the camera, his brows are dark bars just above the eye whites,
under the fringe; `surprised` lifts them clear of the eyes (a gap of skin shows), `firm` makes an inward V over
the eyes, `tired` sags their outer ends (a worried look), `proud` sits one brow higher than the other, the
subtlest of the four. The forelock hangs between the brows and sometimes joins the inner ends. The baby:
`delighted` raises a dark arc above wide eyes, `sulky` a V, `focused` a small lopsided frown, `sleepy` a sag
that reads close to `neutral`. At real size (the full frames) Papa's face is about 20 physical px wide and a
brow is 6-7 px: the four are told apart on the crops, barely at 1:1. Nothing broken seen (no brow inside an
eye, none floating off the head, no stretched bar). The look and the size are Geo's call: tune
`feel.json › characters.expressions` (`browScalePct` first) in the feel panel with `__PP__.setExpressions`.
