The Baby

Explores, grabs, puts things in his mouth, and fakes you out. His gaze and his silence are the tells.

Babbling = safe. A flat line = he's up to something.

From baby's point of view, everything dangerous looks super cool.

Synthesis

INSIGHT · COACH 01 · THE CORE VERB

Read the baby, then pick the right response

READ

his gazethe silencethe criesfalse alarmsfake sleep
➜

RESPOND

parrydodgewaitdo nothingdose it

The parry is one answer among several, which diversifies the game without breaking pillar 1.

Pillar 4 grows too: as the child ages, the right response changes.

INSIGHT · COACH 02 · THE NERVOUS SYSTEM

Noise is the game's nervous system

A single sound economy. Strong, cheap game feel, ideal for the cube prototype.

Baby's noises inform

Silence, cries, false alarms: they tell you what's coming.

Papa's noises threaten

The floorboard, the saucepan: they feed Mom's gauge.

= +

The mobile caveat: plenty of people play muted

So every sound cue needs a visual twin (waves, bubbles) and a haptic twin (a Capacitor vibration on parries and alerts). Respects pillar 3.
